# FRAME lane — changelog bullets and legend lines (for the orchestrator to integrate)

## Changelog bullets (user-facing)

- The anchorage rides at anchor: far hulls fly smaller burgees, far sails melt their emblem into a deeper shade
  of the dye and step back in value, and far hulls gain a pale sheer and a bright waterline lip. The hull you
  hover, select or search for keeps its full flag and full colour.
- Sails are dyed in earth pigments: every coin keeps its hue, at a chroma that belongs to the town's palette.
- Ships sit in the water: a dark waterline, a thin bright lip on the swell side, and a glassy satin slick trailing
  downwind of every anchored hull. Mooring buoys keep a thin ring instead of foam blotches.
- Hulls show a wet band above the boot-top, a weed line and faint salt runs (titans keep their gilt).
- The sea has depth: a deeper dredged channel, sandy shallows, dark sea-grass meadows on the bed, a darker outer
  sea, and clouds drifting across the harbour's mirror. The Shoals stay the lighter turquoise reef.
- At the overview the miniature's sharp row is the Pharos, and the coasts no longer glow.
- Distance: far-off ships and shores settle into the day's haze.
- Swell lines arrive and break along the shore; quay walls and rocks carry a wet run-up line.
- Summer nights: wakes and the swash glow faintly.
- Calm water draws the town's lamps out into longer reflection columns.
- Smoother night skies (no banding).

## Legend lines

- **distance** — Far ships and shores take on the day's haze. Decorative: it never changes with the data, and it
  is not the stale-data fog (which rolls in as low banks over the harbour itself).
- **sea glow** (decorative list) — On summer nights churned water in wakes and the swash glows faintly. Season
  only; never in the Shoals; carries no data.

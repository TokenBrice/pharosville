# Actors: fleet, life, civic and identity

## 1. Scope and method

Research only; no tracked source, assets, configuration or `dist/` changed. Read `AGENTS.md`, the phase brief, onboarding/architecture/runtime/testing docs, and the relevant fleet/life/civic/identity implementations plus Three.js 0.186 upload code. Three exploratory scouts failed at provider capacity before returning findings; the investigation was completed directly.

Six successful real-GPU captures: three overview and three heart, live API/data on the required development origin, `1600×1000@1`, NVIDIA RTX 5070 Ti, quality `high`, approximately 14:00. All six PNGs were opened. No variant was implemented, so **none of the proposed visual equivalence or speedups has been experimentally established**. All timings are **contended**; overview runs in particular varied drastically. Do not interpret their slower medians as an intrinsic overview-vs-heart difference or as production bundle performance.

Capture command template (N = 1, 2, 3; V = overview, heart):

```sh
npm run preview -- --url 'http://localhost:5173/?view=V&t=14&prof=1' \
  --out outputs/need-for-speed/actors/V-N.png --seconds 3 --wait 2500 \
  --steady-profile outputs/need-for-speed/actors/V-N.cpuprofile \
  --heap-sample outputs/need-for-speed/actors/V-N.heap.json \
  --json outputs/need-for-speed/actors/V-N.json --eval '<runtime census>'
```

`overview-1` also used `--profile outputs/need-for-speed/actors/overview-1.boot.cpuprofile`. Its original capture command timed out waiting in the shared lock at 300 s; the successful replacement used 1800 s. Two redundant census captures were cancelled in response to lock-priority instructions, before producing results. No further captures were queued after the explicit capture embargo. Six successful captures, not eight measurements.

Scratch: `outputs/need-for-speed/actors/`. `census.js` shows the final instrumentation: reset existing `window.__harbourProf`, collect 60 engine frames, and temporarily wrap actor render/shadow callbacks to read renderer call-counter deltas. System CPU is inclusive `profile.ms[name]/profile.frames`, measured **before** the separate three-second CPU/heap profiling window. Draws are renderer submissions, not one draw per boat or per internal multi-draw command. Five early censuses accidentally omitted `CrestFlags.group`; the successful overview-1 replacement fixes that. Tables explicitly distinguish the corrected direct-root measurement and additional office meshes.

V8 CPU self time was aggregated from `samples`/`timeDeltas`; generated dev-module line numbers are not reliable repository line numbers, so source references below use the actual source. Heap attribution sums `selfSize` to the nearest fleet/life/civic/identity ancestor, including built-ins and ocean helpers called by actors, then divides by captured frames. These are **sampling-profiler byte estimates**, not retained heap or exact allocations/frame. A 16 KiB sample does not mean one 16 KiB object. Unoptimized numeric boxing/profiler overhead may contribute substantially.

### Implementation ordering recommendation

1. First take **ACTORS-03**, **ACTORS-08**, **ACTORS-09**: local, source-provable no-look wins; pair with targeted existing identity/life/civic tests and one later real-GPU regression set.
2. High-value structural work: **ACTORS-01**, **ACTORS-02**, **ACTORS-04**. They address the real fleet CPU/arrival work, unlike replacing tiny particle systems.
3. Then **ACTORS-05 / 06 / 07 / 10** if profiling after the first group still justifies the complexity. Keep the zero-visual static transform work with S12's single scene-ownership design.

## 4. Ruled out / already shipped / not worth prioritizing

- **'Instance the boats / merge per-boat meshes': already done.** BatchedMesh with per-object frustum culling and `sortObjects=false` at fleet-system295–296, geometry-ID full/LOD swaps756–758, one combined material. A switch to per-variant InstancedMesh is not automatically faster; it adds draw/material complexity and is unmeasured.
- **'Move sails, smoke, flags, folk limbs and gull wings to the GPU': already done.** CPU work is the roots/path/state, not cloth vertices. A full GPU gull trajectory/state machine for14birds is poor first priority.
- **'Bake identity canvas text into an atlas': already done.** Chart SDF/crest assets are static; the expensive work is placement search and sight tests. Civic boards are already change-keyed and atlas-batched. Do not reduce legibility or semantic labels to fix a non-existent redraw loop.
- **'Defer identity/life until after first frame': already implemented.** Engine17,126–165,209–212. The original brief's746ms identity figure does not mean removing it saves746ms firstFrameMs at current default settings. It still competes with arrival/crossfade and responsiveness after that first render.
- **'Merge civic static geometry': already implemented**, civic-system199–200 and assemble108–135; offices are also merged after dressing. Improve copy/memory or spatial organization, not claim thousands of civic draw calls disappear.
- **Blind `frustumCulled=true` on all actor meshes is incorrect.** Smoke/drift carry world origins in custom attributes; folk/gulls move with instance transforms; base quad/geometry bounds may not contain shader-displaced content. Conservative bounds or region chunks require explicit envelopes and may add draws. Main fleet already per-instance culls on the renderer side; CPU posing is the remaining gap.
- **Blanket offscreen wake suppression / stopping the authoritative sim:** excluded without proof of wake-map coverage and unchanged rendered semantics. Contact/wakes are a separate system and offscreen origins can influence visible water/shadows.
- **Scratch-vector pooling everywhere:** existing fleet/worker math scratch makes this mostly shipped. Heap-profiler numeric samples are not evidence of a new Vector3 per boat. Only pool the specific event-transition or container allocations actually present.
- **Removing drift/gulls/folk/labels, shrinking flags indiscriminately or cutting boats:** out of scope/no-scenery-impact requirement; not recommended.
- **GPU fleet matrix row surgery as a first change:** the entire data+matrix pair is only~0.15MB/frame; multiplying GL calls can cost more than the transfer. Early cull/update dirtiness is simpler and should precede private BatchedMesh changes.
- **Static wreck poses:** `wrecksPosed` already keeps static wreck matrices from being rebuilt every ordinary frame; the469buoys and live hulls are the continuing animation work.

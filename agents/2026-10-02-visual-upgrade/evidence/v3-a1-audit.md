## Design audit

1. **Minor protocol defect — `--draw-census` is inert under `--reduced`.** Protocol step 4's two Day/Night still commands include both `--reduced` and `--draw-census`. `scripts/pharosville/preview.mjs:488` refreshes the draw-owner census only `if (args["draw-census"] && !args.reduced)`; under `--reduced` the printed census (line 604) is whatever the app last computed, and `printDrawOwnerCensus` prints "owner census unavailable" when null (`:2229`). The commands still run (flag accepted), but census evidence in exactly those two arms is weaker than implied. Fix: drop `--draw-census` from the reduced stills; take the census in the non-reduced pacing arm or a separate run.
2. **Wording slip in observed-execution:** "3,872 unchanged triangles" — the probe's `triangles: 3872` is the probe pine's **total** triangle count; 320 faces were affected (normals recomputed, 168 verts moved). Should read "3,872 total triangles, 320 faces affected."
3. **Cosmetic cite drift:** "Flowing Date … (`preview.mjs:2007-2023`)" — `installFlowingDate` spans ~2013-2027; the substantive claim (installed only `if (clock && !fixture)`, `:359`) is correct.

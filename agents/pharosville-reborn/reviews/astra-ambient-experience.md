# Astra — ambient experience

## Verdict

PharosVille is a continuously animated instrument with occasional sightings, not yet a place with a day’s worth of life. It already has genuine shared weather, long ship rests, seasonal trees, arrivals, and an idle tour: the failure mechanism is **competing recurrence without an attention hierarchy**, plus rare events that most daytime visits cannot encounter. The useful references are Cloud Gardens’ patient material change, Proteus’ place-bound discovery, and Wind Waker’s anticipation of an approaching destination—not idle-game rewards or a permanent cinematic camera. Rhythm **2/5**, surprise **1/5**, local rest **3/5**, whole-frame rest **1/5**: things happen, but little develops into a memorable before/during/after.

### What watching actually does today

Baseline: ordinary live local time, motion enabled, foreground tab, onboarding dismissed, no selection or open overlay; elapsed time starts after loading. These are source-derived expectations, not a three-hour observation. Pinned `#t=` reference captures cannot demonstrate time progression.

- **Minute 1:** the entrance eases toward rest over nine seconds (`src/systems/garden-arrival.ts:3-32`). Shared breath repeats every nine seconds, gust fronts every 24 seconds; wind direction combines 41/97/241-second periods and breeze an 83-second period (`src/systems/weather.ts:74-82,142-169`). Fleet legs have 90–180-second identity ranges, with geometry-dependent extensions and several-minute rests (`src/systems/motion-planning.ts:300-326,697-749`). A ship already near a boundary may dip its sail and briefly show a nameplate; these are route-clock ceremonies, not new market transactions (`src/systems/garden-arrival-beats.ts:29-71`). Fire/smoke, water and the fleet supply continuous life. No guaranteed narrative event.
- **Minute 5:** after two untouched minutes, four postcards loop, each 36 seconds: 28 seconds traveling, eight seconds nominally dwelling (`src/systems/garden-attract.ts:6-8,28-44`). Even dwell adds drift and a 5% push-in (`src/systems/observe-tour.ts:253-262`). One 144-second circuit has completed by 4:24; the next is underway. Pointer movement restarts eligibility; an open legend or selection can prevent this entirely (`src/pharosville-world.tsx:637-665`). Several ships may have changed leg/rest state, but no global quiet interval is scheduled.
- **Minute 30:** roughly 11 complete postcard loops since activation, 200 breaths and 75 origin gust cycles. Routes revisit existing stops; open-water itineraries choose among two or three anchors (`src/systems/motion-planning.ts:1029-1042`). At noon, lighting presets remain on their daytime plateau: daylight is one between 08:00 and 16:30 (`src/three/garden-day-cycle.ts:210-222`); sun direction is a separate evolving channel. Market refresh can alter states, but polling is producer-dependent, not a thirty-minute programme (`src/hooks/use-api-query.ts:40-45`).
- **Hour 3:** approximately 74 postcard loops and 450 gust cycles. A noon-start session has not reached dusk and has **no scheduled almanac sighting**: exactly one event is selected daily, starting around 01:00, 18:00 or 19:00 (`src/systems/garden-almanac.ts:26-73`). Heron/lantern windows last 24 minutes; meteor eligibility lasts 14.4 minutes, though its visible animation lasts ten seconds after activation (`src/three/garden-almanac-dressing.ts:145-201`). Season remains the startup season. At another start hour, twilight and that day’s one selected sighting may occur. Refresh-derived market changes remain contingent, never guaranteed entertainment.

## Findings

**GAP — the camera is the busiest large object.** Attract travels 78% of its cycle, then still moves during “rest.” Endless repetition makes the world feel like an exhibition reel. `noon.png @ central tower and crowded lower-left fleet` has many competing small reads; moving the entire frame compounds that density rather than revealing an event.

**DEFECT — dwell does not join the next leg continuously.** `observe-tour.ts:128-143` constructs subsequent segment starts from unmodified keyframes; `:258-262` ends dwell at a drifted, 1.05× pose. The next segment returns to the base keyframe before traveling. Source establishes a positional/zoom mismatch; perceptual severity is unmeasured. Fix the path representation, not a boundary smoothing patch.

**DEFECT — invisible meteor can lock out attract.** Its envelope reaches zero at ten seconds (`garden-almanac-dressing.ts:188-194`), but `gardenAlmanac.activeEvent` remains non-null throughout the 14.4-minute window and excludes attract (`pharosville-world.tsx:642`). A visible ten-second event can therefore reserve attention for another fourteen minutes. Arrival age also restarts on activation rather than reflecting a shared astronomical occurrence.

**GAP — seasonal implementation exists; the experiential season is weak.** Autumn crowns genuinely shipped (`src/three/garden-rim-mesh.ts:903-921`), visibly orange at `noon-wholemap.png @ northeast tree strip`; do not propose “add autumn colour” again. The separate seasonal dressing is spring-only, 48 tiny 0.34×0.16 quads near the engawa (`garden-seasonal-dressing.ts:22-60,73-87`). September gets no falling-leaf event. Season is resolved at scene construction (`src/three/world-renderer.ts:869`), not a living phenology system.

**GAP — weather is modulation, not a weather front with a life cycle.** Storm intensity is PSI-gated, wind never completely subsides, and gust frequency never varies (`weather.ts:150-171`). At extreme storm levels the nine-second lightning slots can contain frequent double flashes (`:258-272`), a poor second-monitor relaxation mode. Calm is not “no motion,” but recurring accents need silence between them.

**GAP — events are sparse in time and weak in composition.** Heron descent takes seven seconds then rests; the lantern round is seven tiny spheres lighting sequentially, not a keeper visibly walking (`garden-almanac-dressing.ts:149-184`). `dusk.png @ tower versus surrounding shore` reads principally as changed illumination, not an evening ritual. `night.png @ island and surrounding water` achieves a much stronger luminous hierarchy; preserve it.

**Clock reliability risk, not a proven frozen-clock defect:** `use-world-time-controls.ts:80` samples local hour on React render and owns no clock tick; `use-garden-almanac.ts:21-34` schedules UTC midnight only. Rendering consumes that sampled hour (`use-world-render-loop.ts:578`). Ordinary updates may keep it advancing, but time progression should not depend on unrelated renders. Instrument this boundary during implementation rather than claiming that every three-hour session freezes.

## Ranked ideas

Costs below are **incremental planning estimates, not measurements**: draws / triangles / textures / frame-time target. S ≤8h, M 8–24h, L 24–48h, XL >48h. All preserve deterministic static reduced motion, zero RAF, analytical DOM/ledger parity and global ceilings.

1. **STEP CHANGE — one harbour score, with real silence.** Add a pure director alongside `garden-almanac.ts`; consume it in weather, arrivals and attract. Author 4–6 modest environmental beats/hour, at most one foreground beat, separated by 6–12-minute genuinely quiet intervals. Market events pre-empt decorative accents, never truth. Long periods of ordinary water are the piece, not downtime. **Driver:** clock + separately typed Pharos transitions. **Cost:** 0/0/0, ≤0.15ms; XL. **Risk:** synthetic activity mistaken for data. **Displaces:** metronomic accent scheduling, not shared wind physics; re-pin Stillness’s four-postcard regime. **Dependencies:** fleet motion, ambient life, data lane.

2. **STEP CHANGE — consequential market stories.** On a verified depeg/risk transition, let the affected ship leave its familiar anchorage, traverse a legible corridor, and settle in the existing destination risk water; recovery reverses the relationship, not necessarily the path. A large *verified* issuance gets a restrained quay pennant-opening/receiving ceremony, never fireworks. Do not animate each refetch or infer transactions from rolling aggregates. **Driver:** Pharos, previous/current timestamped observations with provenance. **Cost:** 0–2/≤2k/0, ≤0.25ms; XL. **Risk:** false causality, collision, hiding distressed assets. **Displaces:** three-second tack-out (`motion-planning.ts:54-60`) and competing arrival accents; retains every eligible hull. **Dependencies:** data event contract, fleet routes/visuals, ledger.

3. **STEP CHANGE — a lived evening, not a filter.** Replace the daily lottery’s lantern spheres with one small keeper following the existing rim path, lighting existing fixtures sequentially for 2–4 minutes; nearby birds settle, station work quiets, fire assumes night duty. At dawn the sequence reverses without replaying yesterday’s event. **Driver:** local wall clock. **Cost:** 1–2/≤3k/0, ≤0.15ms; L. **Risk:** silhouette imperceptible or culturally generic ornament. **Displaces:** seven almanac points and dusk bird sorties, never beacon dominance. **Dependencies:** shore/path, life/light, sky; re-pin event selection and dressing tests.

4. **Stationary windows; journeys by exception.** Rewrite attract as 3–6-minute locked compositions, 20–35-second transitions, at most four relocations/hour. Compose one shore vignette with foreground leaves, mooring, empty water and distant tower. Optional “sail with this ship” stays user-triggered; cap automatic following at one rare director beat. Retain manual Observe’s explanatory purpose. **Driver:** clock; event-aware framing only when enabled. **Cost:** 0/0/0, ≤0.05ms; L. **Risk:** boredom if composition is weak. **Displaces:** endless 144-second loop and dwell creep; fix discontinuous segment endpoints. **Dependencies:** camera, garden director, fleet.

5. **Weather chapters with one coherent front.** A 6–10-minute passage: wind darkens one water region, a restrained rain veil reaches roofs, rain diminishes, wet surfaces persist. Use one seeded environmental schedule; PSI still owns stress intensity, never imply rainfall is market data. **Driver:** decorative clock, explicitly separated from Pharos stress. **Cost:** 1–2/≤4k/0–1, ≤0.7ms; XL. **Risk:** transparency/fill cost, conflated weather meanings. **Displaces:** routine gust accents and existing ripple/foam emphasis during rain; reopen sea-vocabulary invariant explicitly. **Dependencies:** water, sky, material/perf lanes.

6. **Make stale fog an arrival, not another haze layer.** Existing semantic haze already exists (`epistemic-haze.ts:15-29`; `world-renderer.ts:4079-4081`). On a freshness edge, move its bounded bank across the affected waters over 30–60 seconds, settle, then clear on recovery; caption names the exact stale feed. Never cover all ships. **Driver:** Pharos freshness. **Cost:** 0/0/0, ≤0.1ms; M. **Risk:** hides analytical cues. **Displaces:** decorative mist in that region, not current haze semantics. **Dependencies:** sky/water, data/UX.

7. **Microseasons, not faster years.** Keep real Northern UTC seasons, but derive blossom opening, petal fall, autumn leaf release and bare winter days from bounded calendar phases. One visible maple branch sheds a few leaves during a director beat; reuse the spring particle budget, not another confetti cloud. **Driver:** calendar. **Cost:** 0–1/≤2k/0, ≤0.15ms; L. **Risk:** geography mismatch, novelty wears out. **Displaces:** constant spring drift; preserve shipped crown palettes. **Dependencies:** vegetation, seasonal geometry lifecycle; re-pin spring-only dressing.

8. **A calm “Now” sentence.** One persistent optional line: “Evening rounds · readings current” or “USDC moved to Watch water · observed 18:42.” Click opens the matching explanation; no ticker, auto-announced decorative chatter or fake precision. **Driver:** director + verified data. **Cost:** 0/0/0 WebGL, negligible steady CPU; M. **Risk:** prose becomes an untrusted narrator. **Displaces:** ambiguous decorative captions and redundant status copy. **Dependencies:** UI, data, accessible ledger.

9. **Repair event lifetime and fairness.** Separate occurrence time, visual envelope, lingering evidence and attention reservation. Meteor reserves ten seconds, not fourteen minutes; heron’s perch remains after its landing releases attention. Rotate eligible small-ship ceremonies instead of permanently preferring market cap (`garden-arrival-beats.ts:86-114`). **Driver:** clock/route, not transactions. **Cost:** 0/0/0, ≤0.1ms; M. **Risk:** over-scheduling. **Displaces:** window-wide camera exclusion and six simultaneous headline plates; re-pin event/arrival tests. **Dependencies:** camera, fleet, UI.

10. **A reliable temporal spine.** Explicit low-frequency visible-tab wall-clock subscription plus UTC date rollover; seeded calendar state and animation elapsed time remain different domains. Hidden tabs resume current conditions without replaying missed ceremonies; reduced motion receives one static authored state. **Driver:** clock. **Cost:** 0/0/0, negligible steady CPU; M. **Risk:** timers accidentally invalidate renderer or wake reduced-motion RAF. **Displaces:** incidental render-driven hour refresh; date-only event hook ownership. **Dependencies:** runtime architecture, accessibility.

11. **Moon phases, but no competing tide.** Shade the existing moon and modulate its existing road through a calendar phase; preserve a readable night minimum without pretending it is astronomical irradiance. Do **not** raise the sea twice daily: the existing tide datum deliberately means weekly supply change (`garden-tide-line.ts:26-53`). **Driver:** calendar. **Cost:** 0/0/0, ≤0.05ms; M. **Risk:** new-moon loss of composition. **Displaces:** invariant full-strength lunar presence; re-pin night composition, retain beacon/road hierarchy. **Dependencies:** sky/water.

12. **Optional sound, never required.** Favour three original/licensed, seamless water/wind/wood-creak recordings plus sparse local bird details over continuous “Japanese” music. Web Audio gain/filter/panning follows existing weather and director; ≤6 concurrent voices, ≤5MB compressed download and ≤24MB decoded buffers. Explicit Sound-on gesture creates/resumes context; default mute, independent volume, suspend hidden tabs, no market alarm noises. **Driver:** clock/weather, not financial urgency. **Cost:** 0/0/0, target ≤0.3ms audio CPU; L. **Risk:** loop fatigue, licensing, office interruption. **Displaces:** visual-only insistence that every small event must attract the eye. **Dependencies:** UX/audio owner; motion preference never implies audio consent.

## Rejected

- Accelerated default day/year: abandons the operator’s wall-clock premise; cadence can improve without falsifying time.
- Daily-login rewards, unlocks and streaks: turn a refuge into an obligation.
- Fireworks on supply growth: growth is not inherently good; spectacle competes with the beacon.
- More birds/fireflies/petals: violates count restraint and treats attention scarcity as asset scarcity.
- Animated crate throughput from aggregate flows: invents a rate; cargo intentionally describes state (`garden-cargo-tide.ts:60-66`).
- Permanent auto-follow and ambient camera noise: remove a second-monitor viewer’s stable visual anchor.

## Cross-lane notes and evidence limits

Highest-leverage package is **director + composed stationary windows + truthful market transitions + evening ritual**, not twelve independent effects. Data lane owns event truth and parity; camera owns continuity; life/shore own choreographed subjects; atmosphere/water own the shared weather implementation. Reserve ≤8 extra draws, ≤15k triangles and ≤2 textures for this package before optional rain/audio; do not add individual worst cases blindly.

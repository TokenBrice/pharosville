# Camera and composition review

## Verdict

The 0.72 rest is an effective inventory view but not a memorable garden composition: at 1600×1000 the Pharos is almost dead-centre (tower x≈48%, crown y≈14%; island x≈40–60%, y≈36–62%), while fleet fragments press every edge and the named harbours mostly sit outside the picture. The eye enters at the gold lantern, runs down the tower, is pulled into the crowded left flotilla, then exits into comparatively empty right water; neither thirds (33/67%) nor golden sections (38/62%) carry the hero, although the upper keep roughly meets the horizontal golden section. Orthographic 45° azimuth/30° elevation makes a clear diagram, but removes parallax, scale recession, a horizon and almost all sky, shortening rather than flattering the tall tower. At 1200×640 the same 0.72 zoom preserves crown, Mole and a 128px right gutter by contract, but height is so scarce that the island’s near cliff can fall under the 80px footer reserve; it is a technically legal frame, not an authored second composition (`camera.ts:85-116`; `camera.test.ts:155-181`).

## Findings

- **GAP — one heraldic centre, little pictorial tension.** Noon/morning/dusk share the same near-centred tower, dense left field, open right water and edge cuts; lighting changes hierarchy but not composition (`morning.png`, `noon.png`, `dusk.png` @ full frame). Night is the best of the four because dark water suppresses hull noise and the lantern becomes unequivocally primary (`night.png` @ centre/right water). No frame shows a horizon or sky band.
- **GAP — “harbour view” hides the harbour ring.** The rest explicitly seats Pharos→Ethereum Mole and reserves right-side *ma*, not two or more legible station precincts (`camera.ts:89-105`). In the frames, only clipped waterfront fragments at left, lower-left and lower-right read; flags/hulls, not harbour architecture, dominate (`noon.png` @ left and bottom edges). The tower is central rather than at a thirds/golden-section power point.
- **GAP — ortho prevents the requested step change.** The renderer fixes camera position to equal x/z offsets and height `distance·sqrt(2/3)`, hence azimuth 45°, elevation 30°, then looks at ground (`world-renderer.ts:5024-5042`). Affine projection keeps every hull at equal apparent scale, collapses foreground/background separation and makes verticals diagrammatic (`projection.ts:44-79`; `garden-observatory-slice.ts:385-394`). It shows the tower’s useful three-quarter faces, but the high view foreshortens its shaft and exposes roofs more than sky.
- **GAP — zoom history optimized coverage, not a shot.** The retired ~0.60 plate showed the map as a small object; 1.0 made the world feel small and forced zooming; 0.72 restored about twice the 1.0 area (`camera.ts:29-39`; `projection.ts:134-142`). That compromise is rational UX, but the result is still a carpet at rest. Tests pin containment and broad centre ranges (43% to right gutter; vertical 38–73%), not focal hierarchy or two-harbour visibility (`camera.test.ts:60-98`). Several test comments still incorrectly call rest 1.0/0.8 (`camera.test.ts:75-78,127-130`): **DEFECT — stale self-description**.
- **GAP — arrival is translation plus scale, not revelation.** Nine seconds move from `target +(-72,+48)` at 82% zoom with a quintic ease (`garden-arrival.ts:3-32`). Azimuth/elevation never change, so it feels like a long 2D slide rather than descending through atmosphere to discover the beacon.
- **DEFECT — resize can undo itself during arrival.** Resize immediately clamps the displayed pose to new bounds (`use-canvas-resize-and-camera.ts:367-398`), but the next arrival sample is still interpolated from the old cached `from/to` (`:657-670`). A resize during the nine-second intro can jump back toward the obsolete viewport composition.
- **GAP — attract is not yet a postcard book.** Its four frames are: **Pharos precinct** (tower mid-height + quay water, 1.15); **Ethereum Mole** (quay + east approach, 1.1); **NE arc** (warning notch + watch bay, 1.0, explicitly promised together only at 1568px); **Wreck Shoal** (inlet + grave scatter, 1.15) (`garden-attract.ts:28-45`). They are sensible subjects, but 28 of every 36 seconds is travel and the remaining dwell adds a 5% push plus tangent drift, so there is almost no still “postcard” (`garden-attract.ts:6-8`; `observe-tour.ts:68-71,253-263`). Tests assert only determinism, zoom band and centre-on-plate—not that subjects fit at either gate (`garden-attract.test.ts:10-29`). `dusk-close.png @ whole frame` demonstrates the generic close-frame failure: half empty glare, accidental edge cuts, no strong hero.
- **GAP — Observe informs but does not direct cinematography.** It chooses lighthouse, leading risk, largest supply change and highest HHI (`observe-sequence.ts:58-118`), then gives every beat the same 12s/3.5s spline+dwell grammar (`observe-tour.ts:63-71`). Subject kind, travel heading, nearby context and data severity do not shape shot size or screen placement.
- **GAP — follow-a-ship is tracking, not cinema.** It predicts a lead tile and continuously recentres it with the current zoom (`use-canvas-resize-and-camera.ts:738-773,816-839`). No trailing screen offset, contextual destination, wake-axis framing or automatic zoom makes the voyage legible; ortho eliminates chase parallax.
- **GAP — whole-map is a debug atlas pretending to be a picture.** `noon-wholemap.png @ full frame` is left-heavy, surrounded by cream, exposes the hard diagonal plate/backdrop seam and sends the eye from tiny tower to flotilla to dead space. It is useful for topology, never beautiful. Whole-map framing is intentionally the computed explicit minimum (`projection.ts:155-177`); it should become an actual map surface or leave the experience.
- **GAP — all seven frames reveal one composition.** `noon-legend.png` visually differs little from noon: edge chrome does not obscure the tower but reinforces “dashboard screenshot.” `dusk-close.png` and `noon-wholemap.png` expose the backdrop seam; the four rest hours repeat the same camera. The system has modes, not a visual grammar.
- **Strength — viewport/reduced-motion discipline is sound.** The monotonic size gate admits 900×720 or 1200×640 without orientation mistakes (`viewport-gate.ts:11-35`). Attract is correctly excluded for reduced motion, selection, Observe, dialogs, events and hidden tabs (`pharosville-world.tsx:637-665`); input freezes the exact current pose (`use-canvas-resize-and-camera.ts:904-908`). Preserve these invariants.

## Ranked ideas

1. **[STEP CHANGE] Adopt a 22° long-lens perspective rig at ~24° elevation.** Replace the view camera in `world-renderer.ts`; extend camera pose beyond `{offsetX,offsetY,zoom}` in `camera.ts`; make `projection.ts` the shared cached view/projection + ray/ground unprojection; route elevated anchors through it in `garden-observatory-slice.ts`. Why: mild scale recession, visible sky, heroic tower, real foreground/background, without wide-angle distortion. **Cost:** 0 draws/tris/tex; ≈<0.15ms CPU/GPU; XL (5–8d). **Risk:** interaction drift, billboards/water assumptions, composition churn. **Displaces/re-pins:** reverse fixed-ortho invariant; replace affine expectations in `camera.test.ts`, projection and `garden-observatory-hit-testing.test.ts`. **Consequences:** `use-canvas-resize-and-camera.ts` pan/zoom/follow operate on target+dolly; `garden-observatory-hit-testing.ts` must project actual 3D bounds and pointer rays, not multiply cached affine rectangles (`:471-482`); DOM anchors use the same matrix; `world-renderer.ts` post camera type changes; directional **shadow camera stays orthographic and world-bounds fitted** (`world-renderer.ts:3894-3932`) but must be regression-checked at orbit extremes. Dependencies: water (view rays/fresnel), sky/horizon, UI labels, render/perf.

2. **[STEP CHANGE] Author a real rest shot, not a zoom constant.** Frame tower near upper-right golden intersection (~62% x, 38% y at island base), Ethereum Mole plus one south/east harbour, a clipped dark foreground pine/torii, and an open-water corridor; store separate 900×720 and 1200×640 safe compositions. Why: one glance says “garden harbour” and gives entry→tower→harbours→water→foreground return path. **Cost:** 0/0/0, 0ms; L (3–5d art direction/captures). **Risk:** fewer simultaneously visible ships. **Displaces:** 0.72 as universal premise, not whole-map access. Re-pin `camera.test.ts` to screen-region subject constraints and foreground clipping; depends rim/vegetation and fleet density.

3. **Curated hourly postcard book.** Give named morning/noon/dusk/night shot sets (Pharos Dawn, Mole Market, Storm Passage, Wreck Memorial), each with subject safe rectangles at both gates, 12–18s genuine holds, 6–10s transitions, and chrome fade; choose set by wall-clock phase. **Cost:** 0/0/0; <0.05ms; L. **Risk:** weak shots when live fleet placement overlaps. **Displaces:** current four coordinates and 28/36 travel; re-pin `garden-attract.test.ts` to subject visibility, phase choice, reduced-motion static first card. Dependencies sky/light, ambient life, UI.

4. **Perpetual 90–140s camera breathing: yaw ±2°, elevation ±1°, dolly ±1.5%.** Pause instantly on input/selection and freeze one canonical pose for reduced motion. Why: foliage, tower tiers and near hulls separate gently; the world feels alive without “camera noise.” **Cost:** 0/0/0; ~0.05–0.2ms plus sustained normal-motion frames; M. **Risk:** seasickness and hit-target churn. **Displaces:** Observe’s constant 5% push/tangent drift; repin scheduler/reduced-motion and hit-target matrix parity. Depends idea 1, perf.

5. **Make follow a cinematic voyage mode.** Place ship on lower-left third with velocity lead, destination harbour on upper-right third; adapt dolly to route length, bias along wake, ease out at berth, offer “return to garden.” **Cost:** 0/0/0; <0.1ms; L. **Risk:** edge routes/clamps, rapidly turning ships. **Displaces:** exact-centre `followTile`; repin `camera.test.ts` follow semantics and hook follow cancellation. Dependencies fleet motion, UI caption parity.

6. **Turn whole-map into a deliberate chart.** On explicit zoom-out, transition to near-top-down ortho, suppress atmospheric backdrop seam, simplify fleet, show water names/harbour marks/legend; otherwise remove the zoom rung. Why: a map can be informative where a tiny diorama cannot be beautiful. **Cost:** 0–3 draws, 0–5k tris, 0–1 tex, <0.3ms; L. **Risk:** mode discontinuity. **Displaces:** `noon-wholemap` scenic claim; re-pin `minZoomForViewport`, semantic LOD, pointer thinning/keyboard parity. Dependencies data/UI/sky.

7. **Lower the existing ortho rig to 24–26° as the fallback if perspective is rejected.** Expose a shallow sky band and lengthen tower silhouette; recalculate world-y projection and framing. **Cost:** 0/0/0, 0ms; M. **Risk:** roof/flag overlap and map footprint. **Displaces:** 30° projection constant; re-pin projection/hit tests and shadow visibility. Dependencies sky, harbour flags.

8. **Shot-aware Observe grammar.** Lighthouse gets slow reveal; risk gets restrained push; supply change tracks ship-to-harbour; concentration holds a two-subject frame. Add per-beat shot descriptors instead of one universal zoom/spline. **Cost:** 0/0/0; <0.05ms; L. **Risk:** data-selected edge subjects. **Displaces:** equal segment grammar; re-pin `observe-tour.test.ts` behavioral transitions. Dependencies data informativity, UI copy.

9. **Responsive camera continuity.** On resize, rebuild active arrival/tour endpoints from normalized subject anchors; preserve centre only for manual camera. **Cost:** 0/0/0; negligible; S–M. **Risk:** resize oscillation. **Displaces:** clamp-only resize; add focused arrival-resize regression test in hook suite.

10. **Composition guardrails, not pixel snapshots.** Define safe-region assertions for tower crown/base, ≥2 harbour anchors, open-water quota and intentional foreground edge intersection at both gates. **Cost:** 0 runtime; M. **Risk:** brittle numeric art direction. **Displaces:** stale/broad centre-range assertions in `camera.test.ts`; extends `garden-attract.test.ts`. Dependencies final shots.

## Rejected

- **Wide-angle 35–50° perspective:** miniature distortion and edge stretching would make the harbour less restful.
- **Free user orbit:** destroys authored flags, labels, shadow/readability and turns the garden into an editor.
- **Keep ortho and tune only 0.72→0.68/0.8:** repeats the failed 0.6/0.72/1.0 loop without adding depth or hierarchy.
- **Use `noon-wholemap.png` as an attract card:** seam, tiny hero and cream void are irredeemable as a scenic shot.
- **Camera shake/handheld noise:** contradicts contemplative stillness; breathing must be deterministic and sub-perceptual.

## Cross-lane notes

Sky/horizon must supply a credible band before lowering elevation. Water must convert ortho-specific view/fog math to the new perspective matrix. Rim/vegetation should nominate one dark foreground mass that tolerates deliberate clipping. Fleet lanes must reserve clear corridors in hero shots and expose route heading/destination to cinematic follow. UI should fade nonessential chrome in attract while retaining analytical/accessibility parity. Render/perf should measure sustained breathing and confirm shadow caching does not re-render merely because the view camera moved.

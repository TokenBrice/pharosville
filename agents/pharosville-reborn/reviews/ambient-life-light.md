# Ambient life and light review

## Verdict

At the 1600×1000 rest framing, ambient life is mostly **paid-for microgeometry rather than perceived life**: tenders are visible as small hulls, and the beacon flame/lantern points read, but birds, pigeons, koi, fireflies, smoke and the waterfall cannot be reliably identified in any reference still. Motion may reveal some of them, yet their 0–4 px bodies have neither the scale nor contrast to survive the fleet carpet. Night does **not** achieve the claimed “dominant beacon, secondary moon road, everything ember” composition: the beacon is the brightest local point, but there is no visible air beam, no distinct moving sea sweep, no visible moon/stars, and the broad warm water patch plus numerous ship lamps become an ambiguous aggregate secondary (`night.png @ centre and adjacent water`). The code contains much of the intended machinery; the step change is to consolidate attention into a few large, staged events rather than add counts.

## Ranked ideas

1. **STEP CHANGE — Make the Pharos sweep the night.** Retune the *existing* cone, not a second beam: a narrower high-energy core plus soft noisy volume, opacity roughly 2–2.5× current `.11`, and stronger forward scatter (`garden-lighthouse.ts:createBeamCone`, `garden-day-cycle.ts:updateDayCycle`). In `garden-water.ts`, replace the diffuse beacon caustic blob with a high-contrast travelling ribbon and terminal broken-glitter patch locked to the exact beam bearing. This is the single unmistakable lighthouse gesture and restores hierarchy. **Cost:** +0 draws/tris/tex, estimated +0.1–0.3 ms; M. **Risk:** transparency ordering/bloom wedge. **Displaces:** current broad beacon caustic patch and suppresses route pulses while crossed. Re-pin blurred-night and open-water emissive image budget (beacon exemption explicit). **Depends:** Water + Sky/Post for haze/bloom calibration.

2. **STEP CHANGE — A daily twilight “lantern breath,” not permanent glitter.** Between civil dusk and night, stage the existing two path lamps, selected harbour windows and 14 fireflies in three slow waves: lamps kindle, insects emerge around only one dark garden arc, reflections lengthen, then settle to embers. Reuse almanac fade/event clock and current instances; enlarge firefly quads to a 1.5–2 px minimum via view-scaled points, not higher count. **Cost:** 0–1 draw, 0 tex, <0.1 ms; M. **Risk:** festival look. **Displaces:** seven-point `lantern-round` event and ship-lantern halos during the 25–40 s beat; preserves 16-lane cap. **Depends:** Garden director + Water; reduced motion gets one composed “just lit” state.

3. **Readable fauna by silhouette, not census.** Collapse microflocks into 3–5 birds at 8–14 px: one slow heron landing/perching on the camera-side rock, two gull pairs crossing open sky, remaining birds visibly perched. Reuse the existing heron geometry/event but make it a regular dusk beat; redistribute, never raise, the total bird count. **Cost:** likely −2 draws by merging ship/summit batches, similar tris, <0.05 ms; M. **Risk:** cartoon scale. **Displaces:** ship-top gull flocks first, then most invisible quay instances. Re-pin sortie-share/count invariants. **Depends:** Fleet for uncluttered sky lanes, Shore/Rim for perch.

4. **Put four koi in a pond the camera can read.** Explicitly reverse the 2026-09-07 decision that emptied the Pharos reflection basin: enlarge/clarify the existing pond to ~35–50 px and let only 1–2 of the same four koi cross its reflection at once, 8–12 px long; the others remain under shade. This buys a canonical Japanese-garden close read without adding fauna. **Cost:** +0 draws/tex, trivial tris, <0.05 ms; M. **Risk:** damages tower/moon mirror. **Displaces:** continuous pond reflection where fish cross and the invisible engawa-shallows placement. **Depends:** Island/Water; re-pin the pond’s “empty secondary read” invariant.

5. **Turn the waterfall into a real near-field focal event.** Re-site the same cascade onto a visible camera-side rock cut, widen the vertical drop, add a single instanced spray/mist draw and let the existing wake field form the plunge pool. It should read as falling white water in a still and supply audible-looking calm motion. **Cost:** +1 draw, ~64 particles/~128 tris, 0 tex, 0.1–0.2 ms; M/L. **Risk:** foam becomes a second bright beacon. **Displaces:** current unreadable ribbon and nearby shore-foam accent; cap daytime luminance below sails. **Depends:** Shore/Rim + Water.

6. **Dawn water mist as a composed reveal.** Use the existing nine mist billboards, lower and enlarge three banks over open foreground water at dawn, with parallax-separated opacity and tower/ship occlusion; fade by midmorning. **Cost:** +0 draws/tex, small existing overdraw increase (~0.1–0.4 ms); S/M. **Risk:** repeats beige wash/seam. **Displaces:** midday mist whisper during dawn and one far haze bank; keep lighthouse crown clear. **Depends:** Sky/Atmosphere + Camera.

7. **One wind, visible everywhere.** Drive grass/niwaki, chain flags, sails, smoke lean, waterfall spray and Gerstner bearing from the same gust envelope; use a slow travelling gust front so the eye sees cause propagate shore→flag→water rather than independent sine loops. **Cost:** 0 draws/tex, tiny uniform/CPU work; M. **Risk:** synchronized “breathing.” **Displaces:** private flag/foliage phase oscillators; re-pin reduced-motion zero-time pose. **Depends:** Fleet, Vegetation, Water.

8. **Weather rain that belongs to the whole frame.** Replace Danger Strait’s barely visible shader dashes with one depth-aware oblique rain sheet, existing wake/ripple impacts, darker cloud shadow and occasional existing lightning; risk water can modulate impact density, not confine weather geographically. **Cost:** +1 draw, ~1–2k tris, 0–1 noise tex, 0.3–0.8 ms; L. **Risk:** visual urgency/perf/analytical confusion. **Displaces:** current Danger-only rain term (`garden-water.ts:1039-1053`) and most glitter during rain. **Depends:** Sky/Post + Water/Data for semantic parity.

9. **Restore the authored night sky.** Diagnose why 720 stars and the 7 u moon never appear in `night.png`; seat the moon inside the visible backdrop, cluster stars into a restrained milky band, and give clouds a moonlit rim with large dark gaps. **Cost:** 0 new draws/tex; <0.1 ms; S/M. **Risk:** competes with beacon. **Displaces:** uniform navy upper field; stars remain below moon-road weight. **Depends:** Sky/Camera/Post; re-pin moon-road alignment.

10. **Reflections as broken vertical calligraphy.** Replace fixed-direction circular lane pools with view/normal-aligned broken strokes whose length follows source height and wind; beacon longest, harbour lamps short, ship lamps mostly pointlike. Keep the same 16 lanes and clamp. **Cost:** 0 draws/tex, ~6–10 ALU/lane, estimated 0.2–0.5 ms; M. **Risk:** shader cost/aliasing. **Displaces:** `pool*.55 + streak*.4` discs (`garden-water.ts:1364-1379`). **Depends:** Water/Render performance.

11. **Sea signs become inspection affordances, not permanent unreadable props.** Hide their carving at rest; on water-body hover/keyboard focus, raise one large board or a restrained DOM/world-linked name, while the ledger remains canonical. **Cost:** −1 atlas texture and potentially −1 draw at rest; M. **Risk:** reverses always-present world naming. **Displaces:** seven unreadable cedar faces and their 1024×1792 atlas; re-pin `VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:184-189`. **Depends:** Data/UI + Accessibility.

## Rejected

- More birds/koi/fireflies: explicitly banned and would turn invisible cost into denser noise.
- Additional permanent lanterns: violates the ember hierarchy and empty-terrace invariant.
- A planar reflection pass: ~40 duplicated draws for a result the fused shader can art-direct.
- Photoreal smoke/fire textures: texture/alpha overdraw and style mismatch; enlarge authored toon silhouettes instead.
- Continuous meteor showers/aurora: unrelated spectacle that competes with the market observatory.
- Sea-sign font/anisotropy tuning alone: text cannot become legible when the whole board is ~10 px.

## Cross-lane notes

Water owns the beam landing, ember strokes, rain impacts and koi/pond integration; Sky/Post owns air-volume contrast and celestial visibility; Camera/Composition must protect one open sky corridor and frame the pond/waterfall; Shore/Rim supplies the heron perch and cascade cut; Fleet must temporarily quiet route pulses/ship halos during the twilight and beam beats; Data/UI must preserve ledger parity for any sign or weather semantics. Performance should GPU-profile the lane-loop ALU and alpha overdraw together, not judge draw count alone.

# L15 — Tech stack, performance headroom, budgets

## Verdict

The production renderer is already technically sophisticated; the missing garden quality is primarily an allocation/art-direction problem, not a WebGL ceiling. Current call/geometry caps are not starving the resting picture, but the threshold’s explicit textureless/three-draw rule and nearly exhausted JavaScript allowance constrain a genuine asset/material step change. Fund that change on WebGL, with fresh M5 Pro measurements and the existing transient triangle breach resolved before expanding geometry.

## Evidence

1. **There is headroom, not measured “85% GPU idle.”** Today’s RTX noon capture has 179 calls, 179 geometries, 374,708 submitted triangles, 50 textures, full tier, GPU p50/p95 2.47/3.17 ms, and pacing p95 16.8 ms (`outputs/holistic/day.json:30-56,96-103,1697-1712`). Unused ceilings: 521 calls, 321 geometries, 125,292 triangles, 22 textures. GPU timer duration divided by a vsync interval is not a utilization measurement or an M5 capacity estimate. Moreover this capture already earns DPR 1.5: 3.6 million backing pixels, not native DPR 1.

2. **These are inherited content envelopes, not hardware throughput calibration.** The caps were raised in July for the enlarged fleet, hero assets and sea (`tests/visual/pharosville-performance.spec.ts:35-58`); the actual reference performance gate remains independent. M5 evidence exists, but is dated: September rest about 105 calls/50 textures, earlier completed garden 321–337k triangles and pacing p95 16.8 ms (`docs/pharosville/TESTING.md:549-560`). Today’s overview is only 171 calls/321,616 triangles/43 textures (`outputs/holistic/overview.json:14,35,1626-1641`). The documentation’s “whole-map fully spent at 72” is a historical arm, not today’s observed default (`TESTING.md:549-551,603-613`). Do not treat either as worst-case coverage of 320 ships, selection and transitions.

3. **The scheduler is not currently making the garden cheap-looking.** Noon/night captures reach full. Full is admitted at pacing p90 ≤20 ms and JS submission ≤30 ms; down/up hysteresis is 2/8 frames (`src/renderer/render-scheduler.ts:114-124,241-252`). Camera movement retains measured load quality rather than stripping water detail (`:138-168`); unattended cadence is capped near 60 Hz, then 30 Hz after three minutes (`:16-46`). DPR uses pacing plus GPU-gated supersampling, an 8MP canvas cap, a 1.5 supersample ceiling and 10 ms projected GPU p95 budget (`src/systems/render-surface-budget.ts:1-38,187-225,247-255`). This has real M5 calibration: ungated supersampling previously oscillated at approximately 50 fps (`:18-28`). It is a useful safety system, not a license to spend RTX spare time everywhere.

4. **Confirm H1/H2/H3 visually, refute their attribution to insufficient global rendering technology.** `day.png` (bottom third/top-left) shows an undifferentiated bank and paddle-like pine pads; `overview.png` exposes the plate; `night.png` loses most foreground detail. The threshold uses only 14,788 submitted triangles across three draws (`outputs/holistic/day.json:600-616`) while covering a large screen area. The bible explicitly mandates smooth, textureless, three-draw threshold geometry ≤15k construction triangles (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:47-57`). Meanwhile the renderer already has half-float 4× MSAA, N8AO, bloom, fused grade/LUT/rays/keyline and SMAA (`src/three/garden-post.ts:1346-1369`). Eightfold AO sampling was rejected on measured M5 images as below the temporal noise floor (`:204-247`). H4’s fleet crowding is visible, but increasing per-ship triangles would not resolve its spatial/semantic problem [INFERENCE].

5. **The triangle failure is real and unexplained.** Execution records 566,611 triangles at 145s and 574,545 at 258s, with about 75 extra calls (`agents/2026-10-02-visual-upgrade/02-execution-record.md:83-87`). Those totals exceed 500k by 66,611/74,545, not the record’s stated “216k”; that number may describe growth over baseline [INFERENCE]. Cached static shadows redraw on scene/frustum/sun changes (`src/three/renderer-shadow-rig.ts:423-435`), making an extra render pass a plausible investigation lead, not a diagnosis. Retrying to green does not establish a safe geometry reserve.

6. **WebGPU is not warranted now.** The archived r185 spike (`git show 599c822:agents/2026-07-29-webgpu-spike-report.md`, “Headline measurements”/“Port-surface inventory”) added 44.6 KiB gzip to the default renderer graph, required replacing 1,725 GLSL lines plus nine material patches, and bypassed post; its 60 fps therefore did not prove parity. Current r186 still documents [ShaderMaterial as WebGL-only](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r186/src/materials/ShaderMaterial.js#L20); [WebGPURenderer’s WebGL2 fallback](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGPURenderer.html) does not preserve classic shader support. The removed spike must remain isolated (`TESTING.md:541-547`; forbidden chunks: `scripts/bundle-budgets.mjs:130-134`). Compute could justify a later program, but no required garden improvement here demands it.

## Ranked levers

### 1. Buy garden form/material quality per visible pixel
**Change:** Rebuild `garden-threshold.ts`/niwaki foreground around authored stone/moss/wood grouping, convincing branch silhouette, restrained roughness/normal variation and local baked/contact shading. Start with vertex colour/shared packed noise; preserve batching, but permit meaningful material buckets rather than three featureless surfaces. **Impact 5/5:** directly replaces the largest weak region, without more scenery or motion. **Effort L. Risk:** material noise, lawn brightness, texture bandwidth. **Budget proposal:** initially ≤30 additional calls, ≤60k additional submitted triangles and ≤4 shared textures: approximately 209 calls/435k triangles/54 textures at today’s rest, not a raised global ceiling. These are design allocations, not measured costs; count shadow/reflection submissions too. **Contracts:** challenge bible `:47-57`; preserve hierarchy `:38-45`, attention `:97-112`, colour anchors `:114-122`, DOM parity. **Dependencies:** garden/vegetation art, lighting, asset pipeline. **Verification:** matched preview noon/night/reduced and gate-size captures, material/threshold tests, census, M5 pacing p95 ≤20 ms; accept only obvious whole-frame improvement.

### 2. Calibrate a quality frontier on the actual M5 Pro
**Change:** Use existing preview/texture census and `render-surface-budget.ts` to compare identical DPR1/DPR2 content on M5; keep cadence and hysteresis intact unless measured quality loss warrants change. **Impact 4/5:** turns speculative headroom into safe content funding. **Effort M. Risk:** thermal/noise and timer interpretation. **Numbers:** retain pacing p90/p95 ≤20 ms and startup ≤2.5s; target whole-frame GPU p95 ≤10 ms where trustworthy, not a Metal per-pass timer gate. DPR1.5→2 adds 78% pixels (3.6→6.4MP); linear RTX projection is 3.17→5.64 ms [INFERENCE], with no M5 guarantee. Prefer garden materials over those extra pixels. **Contracts:** `TESTING.md:276-320,505-539`; keep 8MP and no-timer/no-supersampling guards. **Dependencies:** serialized M5 access, final art. **Verification:** serial normal, uncapped knockout, interaction, overview and reduced arms; scheduler/surface-budget focused tests.

### 3. Diagnose and remove transient over-budget work
**Change:** Add event-correlated, threshold-triggered draw-owner evidence around shadow refresh, reflections, environment bake and content replacement; repair the responsible owner/accounting at source. **Impact 3/5:** protects calm and makes additional geometry defensible. **Effort M, diagnosis uncertain. Risk:** mislabelling real transient work as telemetry noise. **Contracts:** retain honest per-frame counts and 500k limit; `TESTING.md:98-103,111-113`; per-second long-session assertions (`pharosville-performance.spec.ts:387-455`). **Dependencies:** renderer lifecycle/shadow owners. **Verification:** focused owner regression plus ≥600s reference stability run without retry-based acceptance; retain trigger-frame census and timing.

### 4. Unblock earned asset bytes, not blanket GPU cap inflation
**Change:** Keep local asset-first delivery; if textured authored assets require the general loader, make one clean loader cutover and selectively rebaseline `bundle-budgets.mjs` after measuring imports/startup. The current tiny loader intentionally supports textureless static geometry (`garden-models.ts:554-571`). **Impact 4/5; effort M** for budget/loader integration, excluding art. **Risk:** decoder/loader graph growth. **Numbers:** 948/963 KiB leaves 15 KiB (`execution-record.md:31`); a provisional 1,024 KiB aggregate cap would leave 76 KiB over that baseline, approximately the existing +8% precedent (`bundle-budgets.mjs:118-127`). Approval must follow measured feature weight, not precede it. Keep raw/per-chunk gates and performance unchanged. **Dependencies:** asset lens, same-origin local media, desktop loading gate. **Verification:** runtime-media, bundle-size, loader tests, blocked-viewport requests and coherent-startup reference checks.

## Do-not-do / traps

Do not fill 700 calls, add foliage counts, enable moving ship shadow casters, or substitute more bloom/AO for garden authorship. Do not repeat rejected A2/B1/P1 micro-craft or unnecessary W1 temporal reduction (`execution-record.md:17-20`). Do not sum Metal pass timers, claim static-frame p95 as animation performance, or revive dual-renderer production flags.

## Invariants worth challenging

Challenge the threshold’s **three smooth textureless draws/15k construction triangles**: it encodes a cheap implementation as visual doctrine. Challenge categorical “do not raise 72 textures” only if a measured authored asset needs it; count and logical bytes are different budgets (today’s census estimates approximately 580 MiB live texture+renderbuffer storage, not measured VRAM: `day.json:887-895`; caveat `TESTING.md:200-204`). Retain rest-seat composition, fleet completeness, static reduced motion, analytical parity, API secrecy and desktop gating.

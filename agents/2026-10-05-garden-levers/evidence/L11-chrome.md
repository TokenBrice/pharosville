# L11 — DOM chrome, typography, UI look & feel

## Verdict
The chrome is restrained, but its strongest visible message is currently a backend error rather than an intelligible market reading. The detail sheet has useful editorial hierarchy; the surrounding search, ledger and changelog still belong to a different, purple-and-gold interface. The step change is a coherent observatory reading layer—not more Japanese-looking ornament or another font.

## Evidence
1. **Confirm hypothesis 5; the interruption is systemic.** `outputs/holistic/day-chrome.png` (bottom-left) shows “Mint and burn unavailable · Fetch failed: Schema validation failed…”; `day-1200x640.png` truncates it, while `day-900x720.png` exposes the technical reason across a raised bottom strip. `src/systems/detail-model.ts:117-130` selects the first non-current source in registry order, removes the clock, and prints its raw reason. It displaces teaching, market transitions and atmosphere by design (`docs/pharosville/CONTRACTS.md:101-105`). This is not merely unfortunate error copy: one failed source monopolizes the single reading channel. It does not prove all market data failed.
2. **The card is calm, but its precision is hidden.** `selected-usdc.png` (upper-right) clearly separates name, Watch Breakwater / −1 bps, narrative and three figures. However, “24h −10.1%” does not identify supply change; the cause of Watch despite near-peg price is not quantified. Labels are deliberately shortened and capped at three (`src/lib/format-detail.ts:315-361`); own DEWS, evidence and observation qualifiers live in folded Currently (`:578-590`). Disclosure remembers its open state for the session (`src/components/detail-panel.tsx:52-84`). This supports calm complexity, but [INFERENCE] a new reader can mistake the percentage for price return and the band for peg deviation alone.
3. **Typography is a sound foundation, not a missing technology.** Three local Garamond faces (400, italic 400, 600) are loaded with swap; system sans measures figures (`src/pharosville.css:1-23,48-61`). Prose has readable leading and pretty wrapping (`:592-597`); figures use tabular numerals (`:620-623`). Small italic labels are only 0.78rem (`:618`), visually delicate in the selected capture. Garamond reads as a Western printed field guide; that is compatible with restraint, but not evidence of Japanese garden literacy. The brief explicitly rejects cultural references as literal decoration (`PRODUCT.md:41-44`).
4. **Material vocabulary is split.** The detail card has dyed paper, procedural grain, double keyline and lift (`src/pharosville.css:494-510`); legend uses paper (`:1133-1148`). Quick-find stays near-opaque purple with gold activation (`:1724-1726,1773-1776`), and ledger/changelog inherit the older purple/gold panel (`:1113-1128,1303-1335`). Ledger adds paper disclosures inside that surface (`:1363-1395`). Those latter appearances are code-derived, not observed expanded captures. Wall-clock paper/ink tokens are already shared (`src/systems/chrome-air.ts:56-93`), so a second theme system is unnecessary.
5. **Restraint becomes ambiguity.** Both gate captures retain readable caption and Explore without a closed-state collision; at 900px the caption moves upward (`src/pharosville.css:1846-1861`). But Explore shows `/` while clicking it expands controls (`src/components/world-controls.tsx:180-190`); pressing `/` opens search (`src/pharosville-world.tsx:1037-1046`). Find, legend and ledger are hidden until approach/focus/input (`src/pharosville.css:805-823`). Previous release already recorded long warning overlap with expanded controls at exactly 1200px (`agents/2026-10-02-visual-upgrade/02-execution-record.md:88-89`). Expanded-state safety is therefore not established by these captures.
6. **Preserve the analytical/accessibility architecture.** Quick-find is a labelled, autofocus combobox with keyboard navigation and live result count (`src/components/quick-find.tsx:30-65,78-127`). Visible and screen-reader ledger share one body and swap rather than duplicate (`src/components/accessibility-ledger.tsx:131-138`; `src/pharosville-world.tsx:1367-1379`). Its seven-source provenance is substantive (`accessibility-ledger.tsx:198-212`). Chips are intentionally selected/arrival-only, collision-aware visual duplicates (`src/components/harbor-label-chips.tsx:77-101,116-149`), not a reason to label every ship. Debug HUD is **not production-default**: preview forces `debug=1`, while app visibility is opt-in (`scripts/pharosville/preview.mjs:439-442`; `src/lib/pharosville-debug.ts:1-12`). Night capture is clean, so it cannot establish night chrome readability. Hypotheses 1–4 concern world composition; chrome changes alone cannot resolve them.

## Levers — ranked
### 1. Separate market reading from source health
**Change:** Refactor now-caption/detail-model into a terse market reading plus a bounded, explicit health disclosure: e.g. “Issuance unavailable · inspect sources,” opening the ledger. Keep technical reasons, seven-source state, coverage and exact times intact there; label held PSI independently instead of implying global health. Fix the live mint/burn ingestion mismatch at its source in the data lane, not by suppressing the warning.

**Impact 5/5:** Restores useful meaning and serenity during partial failure. **Effort M. Risk:** false reassurance; never say “live” or “complete” globally when qualified. **Contracts:** deliberately changes warning precedence (`CONTRACTS:101-105`), preserves source qualification/parity (`:24-46`) and three coarse readings (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS:59-67`). **Dependencies:** API drift owner, L09 reading design. **Verification:** real-GPU preview of live failure plus current/held/unavailable scenarios at both gates; now-caption/detail-model/ledger tests prove all warnings remain reachable and announced without repetitive minute updates.

### 2. One quiet ink-and-sheet system
**Change:** Migrate quick-find, ledger and changelog to existing chrome-air paper/ink roles; keep neutral sans for operational labels/data and Garamond for names/prose. Replace purple/gold nested paper records with typographic groups and fine rules. Remove ornamental grain/seal framing if it does not survive whole-frame review; do not replace it with faux calligraphy. Establish a shared spacing/type scale instead of component-specific sizes.

**Impact 4/5:** Makes inspection feel part of the garden, not an attached fantasy console. **Effort M. Risk:** losing hierarchy or night contrast. **Contracts:** clock-only ≥4.5:1 roles (`CONTRACTS:95-100`), restraint (`PRODUCT:48-57`). No new GPU assets; reuse fonts and lazy panels given 948/963 KiB JS usage (`execution-record.md:31`). **Dependencies:** none beyond shared palette. **Verification:** headed previews of every panel day/night, reduced motion and both gates; chrome-air tests plus actual composited contrast. Existing tests sweep token states, not CSS's 1.2s polarity transition (`chrome-air.test.ts:75-84`; `pharosville.css:510`): inspect that transition separately, without claiming an observed failure.

### 3. Explain the selected reading before the appendix

Keep full provenance below. Organize expanded records into readable evidence groups; pin Close within the scrollable inspector. **Impact 4/5:** Converts a beautiful plaque into an understandable analytical instrument. **Effort M. Risk:** density and duplicated semantics. **Contracts:** first-screen/row bounds and own-risk truth (`CONTRACTS:119-126,135-137`); DOM authority (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS:59-67`). **Dependencies:** L09 exemplar guide, detail formatting. **Verification:** selected USDC, distressed consort, held PSI and chain records in headed captures; detail/format-detail/ledger parity tests; keyboard scroll, disclosure and focus restoration.

Preserve 44px control targets and Stay's ambient quiet. Consolidate duplicate `pv-panel-enter` definitions (`src/pharosville.css:1398-1444`) into one intentional short motion.

**Impact 4/5:** Calm becomes learnable instead of concealed; minimum-size windows stop depending on short copy. **Effort M. ## Do-not-do / traps
- Do not hide failures, loosen schema validation, or translate unavailable values into zero.
- Do not add kanji, brush fonts, vermillion seals or animated paper to manufacture authenticity. Spatial rhythm and legibility are the Japanese-garden contribution.
- Do not add a UI library or remote font/assets; native dialogs, disclosures and combobox behavior already exist.
- Do not treat debug HUD screenshots as shipped default UI or use `--clean` to evaluate chrome: it removes the entire layer.
- Avoid another imperceptible craft pass: A2/B1/P1/W1 were rejected or unwarranted (`execution-record.md:17-20`). Require whole-frame, expanded-state differences—not grain crops.

## Invariants worth challenging
Challenge `CONTRACTS:101-105`'s **exclusive** warning priority: persistent, scoped qualification should coexist with valid readings. Challenge the implementation's “one resting affordance” rule (`src/pharosville.css:719-720`), not PRODUCT's restraint: two unambiguous words can cost less attention than a concealed toolbar. Do not challenge ledger parity, reduced-motion completeness, local assets, colour anchors or desktop admission.

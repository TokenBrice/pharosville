# L18 — External state-of-the-art benchmark

## 1. Verdict

PharosVille has the restraint of a calm harbour but not the material presence of a garden or the immediately decipherable grammar of excellent ambient visualization. The step change is **authored botanical/stone composition + legible data hierarchy + readable night lighting**, not replacing WebGL or increasing spectacle.

## 2. Evidence and reference shortlist

Research used primary project pages, published case studies and static artwork; no external experience was browser-tested. Transfer recommendations and estimated impact/effort are **[INFERENCE]**, not measured performance claims. Publication/release dates are distinguished from continuing older references.

1. **Garden presence: confirm hypotheses 1 and 2, with qualification.** `outputs/holistic/day.png` (bottom third, upper-left bough) shows smooth green swells and solid flattened tree caps, not distinguishable moss/gravel/needle structures. `overview.png` exposes the square plate; however, the rest shot already hides its edges and establishes borrowed hills, so “floating plate” is not the whole rest-frame problem. [Tiny Glade (2024)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2198150/Tiny_Glade/) assembles contextual bricks, pebbles and planks; its [official night artwork](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2198150/ss_c9086858afd32f9c2bd49c164ddc2494af1e7f31.1920x1080.jpg) retains cool-lit paths, differentiated foliage and warm local light. [Garden Life (2024)](https://stillalive.games/our-games/garden-life/) documents painterly rendering and nonidentical procedural plants; its [garden screenshot](https://stillalive.games/images/screenshots/gar_screenshot_01_hu_d4dee05c5afb1f8c.webp) separates stone, timber and multiple plant silhouettes. Transfer material specificity and contextual joins, **not** that screenshot’s decorative density or gardening gameplay.

2. **The strongest contemporary browser comparison is ecological, not automotive.** [Of the Oak (2025), Marshmallow Laser Feast/Kew](https://marshmallowlaserfeast.com/project/of-the-oak/) combines scientific observation, meditations and an inspectable species field guide. [Lusion’s primary case study](https://lusion.co/projects/of_the_oak/) contains a Houdini→WebGL pipeline note: intricate branch/node geometry, custom compression to 3.5 MB and instancing. Its [field-guide artwork](https://marshmallowlaserfeast.com/app/uploads/2025/04/Screenshot-2025-07-02-at-14.14.56-1024x523.jpg) isolates one selected organism beside explanatory text while leaving the ecological whole visible. This is an unusually close model for “poetic whole, precise inspection”; it is **not evidence that the project uses Gaussian splats**.

3. **Modern web craft does not imply a mandatory renderer migration.** [Choo-Choo World](https://lusion.co/projects/choo_choo_world/) demonstrates joyful authored WebGL scenery; a [2026 technical showcase](https://www.webgpu.com/showcase/choo-choo-world-webgl-train-set/) reports shared UV baking for day/night. [Bruno Simon’s Folio 2025 source](https://github.com/brunosimon/folio-2025) documents coordinated day/year/weather, wind, foliage, lighting, instancing and compressed assets. Borrow coordination and economical richness, not the car game. [Active Theory’s 2026 showcase](https://www.webgpu.com/showcase/active-theory-portfolio/) describes immersive Hydra scenes and compressed/lazy assets, but its neon/particles/AI navigation are anti-references here (`PRODUCT.md:35–44`). Official [TSL terrain](https://threejs.org/examples/webgpu_tsl_procedural_terrain) and [compute-water](https://threejs.org/examples/webgpu_compute_water.html) demonstrate programmable fields, not proof that PharosVille needs WebGPU. Its previous spike was NO-GO (`TESTING.md:541–547`).

4. **Calm data art teaches its grammar.** [Giorgia Lupi’s 1,374 Days (2023)](https://www.pentagram.com/work/1-374-days-my-life-with-long-covid) pairs expressive brushstrokes with an explicit legend and additional mark families; beauty does not substitute for explanation. [Stefanie Posavec’s Nesta work (2026)](https://www.stefanieposavec.com/projects/nesta-afs) accumulates thousands of phrases into larger forms: a useful perceptual aggregation reference, but its deliberately blurred qualitative meaning must not become financial ambiguity. Older, continuing benchmarks [earth.nullschool](https://earth.nullschool.net/about) and [Listen to Wikipedia](https://listen.hatnote.com/) respectively separate field/overlay/source cadence, and map addition/subtraction to distinct instruments with edit-size pitch. Transfer redundant, learnable channels—not weather-like particles invented for issuance. **Confirm hypothesis 4 perceptually:** `day.png` (middle-left/back fleet) presents many similar sails, while tower→stability and water→risk are not self-explanatory without instruction (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:59–67`).

5. **Night is not a synonym for absent information.** `night.png` (foreground and island foot) loses material separation; lamp dots remain clearer than the garden. **Confirm hypothesis 3’s night concern; partially confirm noon:** noon is visibly blue-hazed, but the tower itself remains well-separated. Tiny Glade’s night artwork demonstrates cool fill plus warm accents without turning night into day. [Spark’s current Gaussian-splat renderer](https://sparkjs.dev/docs/spark-renderer/) offers LoD and sorting but explicitly documents mostly transparent splats and problematic depth writing. A scanned garden is a research reference, not a safe replacement for time-lit, selectable analytical geometry. Hypothesis 5 was not assessed by this lens.

## 3. Ranked transferable levers

### 1. Author the garden at the scale of the resting image
**Change:** Rebuild `garden-threshold.ts`/`garden-niwaki.ts` around one asymmetrical stone grouping, a readable gravel-to-moss seam, stepping approach and a genuinely branching pine silhouette. Replace existing swells/props rather than adding a themed prop catalogue. Use Of the Oak’s offline-authoring/instanced-detail lesson for a compact reusable botanical kit.
**Impact 5/5:** Makes “Japanese garden threshold” visible before interaction. **Effort L. Risk:** ornament crowding, hull occlusion. **Touched:** authored seat and empty inlet stay; challenge textureless three-draw/15k threshold prescription (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:19–25,47–57`). Global ceilings remain (`ARCHITECTURE.md:90–95`). **Dependencies:** terrain, vegetation, camera, asset pipeline. **Verify:** full-frame/16px-notan day/night at both gates, threshold occlusion tests and draw/texture census; judge unmistakable garden identity, not crop-only detail.

### 2. Make three coarse readings visually learnable
**Change:** In `systems/pharosville-world.ts`, `risk-placement.ts`, fleet rendering and DOM teaching, establish unequal flotillas with protected water corridors; give risk waters discrete boundary/character differences and leaders unmistakable size/silhouette emphasis. Add a compact optional DOM key showing real rendered exemplars of tower, water and sail, with provenance and unavailable states.
**Impact 5/5:** Converts attractive scenery into an observatory. **Effort L. Risk:** formation could conceal members’ own distress. **Touched:** retain every tracked record/ship, own evidence and DOM parity (`CONTRACTS.md:24–26,56–66,107–118`); no permanent ship-label carpet. **Dependencies:** data model, placement, fleet, chrome/accessibility. **Verify:** matched calm/distress/held fixtures, ledger parity tests, unaided five-second reading review, reduced-motion captures.

### 3. Light night’s surfaces, not more lamp dots
**Change:** Reauthor `garden-day-cycle.ts`, `garden-aerial.ts`, `garden-print-inks.ts`: broad clock-owned cool sky fill, controlled material response and localized warm bounce around existing practicals. Establish separate foreground/water/headland planes before grading; preserve noon’s neutral key instead of uniformly washing everything blue.
**Impact 5/5:** Restores a watchable garden and readable data all day. **Effort M. Risk:** counterfeit daylight or glowing fleet competition. **Touched:** challenge night value targets where needed; keep beacon dominance, wall-clock ownership and palette anchors (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:27–36,69–95,114–122`). **Dependencies:** sky, materials, water/post. **Verify:** night-water, tower-air separation, ninths, selected-sail recognition and M5 Pro GPU/tail metrics—not value MAE alone.

### 4. Spend detail on material transitions
**Change:** Use low-frequency moss coverage/roughness, gravel rake direction, stone contact shading, timber grain and sparse needle/leaf edge breakup in threshold/flora materials. Bake static contact information, retaining dynamic solar illumination; reuse packed noise/atlas slots rather than a new texture per species.
**Impact 4/5:** Turns synthetic blobs into tactile, coherent forms. **Effort M. Risk:** shimmer/overdraw and texture-budget overflow. **Touched:** revise textureless rule; whole-map already spends 72 textures (`TESTING.md:549–551`), so replacement is mandatory. **Dependencies:** lever 1 silhouettes, material/asset owners. **Verify:** DPR1/2 full frames, temporal probe, slow camera motion, texture census and resource disposal tests.

### 5. Offer observation, not another idle spectacle
**Change:** Borrow Of the Oak’s field-guide structure and Posavec’s [Taking an API for a Walk](https://www.stefanieposavec.com/projects/v-and-a-storehouse): an opt-in “show me why” journey through the three readings, each camera view paired with exact DOM explanation; return to the authored seat. Reuse existing camera intent/selection in `pharosville-world.tsx` and director authority, not an autonomous second tour clock.
**Impact 4/5:** Teaches while preserving ambient rest. **Effort M. Risk:** narration crowds warnings; museum-style endless browsing would violate the product. **Touched:** idle never tours, attention budget and warning precedence remain (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:19–22,97–112`; `CONTRACTS.md:101–105`). **Dependencies:** camera, chrome, data explanations. **Verify:** keyboard/Escape completion, warning precedence and deterministic reduced-motion states; guided capture plus idle quiet-watch metrics.

## 4. Do-not-do / traps

Do not copy medieval castles, saturated flower abundance, neon, free-driving controls, looping narration, cinematic depth-of-field over analytical objects, or sound-only truth. Do not import a splat runtime or switch renderer to acquire a “2026” label. Do not bake a flattering hour into surfaces. A2/B1/P1 craft changes were visually indistinguishable and W1 unwarranted (`02-execution-record.md:17–20`): require macro-frame improvement before polishing roughness decimals.

## 5. Invariants worth challenging

Challenge **three smooth, textureless threshold draws/15k triangles** and **six fixed pine pads** (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:47–57`): these prescribe the weak artifact rather than its perceptual purpose. Challenge the near-black night table (`:29–36`) if it prevents legibility. Keep negative space, restrained chroma, complete static truth, desktop gate, same-origin API and one renderer. Budget expansions need evidence, not RTX headroom extrapolation.

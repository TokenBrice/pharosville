# L02 — Rendering pipeline vs state of the art

## Verdict
The renderer is already a sophisticated WebGL pipeline; its largest gap is **lighting and surface authorship, not missing fashionable passes**. Spend headroom on coherent daylight/night readability and grounded garden surfaces, then contact-hardening shadows—not a WebGPU rewrite, extra bloom, or a new AO acronym.

## Evidence
1. **Modern, correctly ordered HDR pipeline.** Actual chain: Render → N8AO → additive mip bloom → fused sky-contact keyline, shadow-map shafts, parametric grade, **Khronos Neutral** tone mapping, display-domain LUT/dither → SMAA (`src/three/garden-post.ts:1346–1369,1475–1546`). Composer uses HalfFloat/4×MSAA; canvas AA is disabled; output is sRGB (`garden-post.ts:1436–1439`; `src/three/world-renderer.ts:505–516`). LUT is raw data, encodes/decodes sRGB around its lookup, and final output encodes once (`garden-post.ts:368–405`). N8AO auto-gamma follows `renderToScreen`, so the intermediate AO pass does not introduce another encoding (`node_modules/n8ao/src/N8AOPostPass.js:787–789`). **No observed colour-management defect.** Runtime guide still describes AgX (`docs/pharosville/THREEJS_AGENT_REFERENCE.md:60–64,92–95`); code selects Neutral (`garden-post.ts:60`). Correct that documentation during implementation.
2. **Hypothesis 3 confirmed with qualification.** `outputs/holistic/day.png` (distant fleet/horizon) is blue-grey and heavily veiled, but tower masonry and foreground sunlit hulls have real contrast; it is not uniformly flat. `night.png` (bottom third/headland) approaches black while sky and ridges remain legible. Night fill is only ambient 0.06/hemi 0.1, with moon-dependent direct lighting (`src/three/garden-day-cycle.ts:184–202`). Night AO exponent is 5 versus day 3; vignette remains active (`garden-post.ts:127–164`). LUT adds contrast, not a night floor (`scripts/pharosville/generate-garden-luts.mjs:80–110`). [INFERENCE] These downstream darkening terms amplify, rather than cause alone, inadequate surface illumination.
3. **IBL is present, but not GI.** PMREM sees the same sky material as the camera, not garden geometry (`src/three/garden-environment.ts:38–43`). Differential SH smooths probe swaps and is zero at rest; it supplies no new local bounce (`garden-environment.ts:78–105`). Environment intensity is day 0.55/night 0.12 (`:138–140`). Ambient + hemisphere + sky IBL overlap by design (`:120–129`); the comment claiming day fill 0.57 is stale against actual 0.15 + 0.68 (`garden-day-cycle.ts:143–157`). [INFERENCE] A unified irradiance owner would simplify calibration and give shade stronger spatial character.
4. **Shadows are modern PCF, not PCSS/contact-hardening.** One view-fitted, cached directional map: full 2048, balanced 1024, recovery 768; fixed bias −0.00015/normalBias 0.35 and radius 3→7 with sun elevation (`src/three/renderer-shadow-rig.ts:145–162,350–371,403–440`). Ships never cast; no cascade rig is instantiated (`:7–17,423–427`). Three 0.186 PCF uses five hardware-comparison Vogel samples (`node_modules/three/src/renderers/shaders/ShaderChunk/shadowmap_pars_fragment.glsl.js:148–176`). Softness changes with clock, not blocker/receiver separation. Old packed-depth PCSS snippets are **not drop-in compatible** with this comparison sampler.
5. **AO is cheap but deliberately coarse.** Half-CSS-resolution, Performance quality, radius 2 world units; transparency awareness disabled; zoom/tier/idle fade and overview texture release exist (`garden-post.ts:1443–1466,1592–1644`). Composite AO darkens already-lit scene colour, rather than just indirect irradiance (`node_modules/n8ao/src/EffectCompositer.js:231–259`). Higher-quality AO was already rejected by a documented M5 A/B (`garden-post.ts:204–247`). Do not repeat that sample-count experiment without materially different garden geometry.
6. **Other “missing” techniques mostly already exist.** Half-res 36-step low-sun volumetric shafts, selective mipmapped planar hero reflection, and silhouette-only ink are shipped (`garden-post.ts:512–534,631–687`; `src/three/garden-hero-reflection-pass.ts:22–26,77–95,137–178`). No DOF or temporal colour AA appears in the constructed chain. Reflection rendering explicitly disables shadow sampling (`garden-hero-reflection-pass.ts:173–178`). Hypotheses 1/2 are visually supported by `overview.png` (square plate), `day.png` (smooth bank/crude pine); **post cannot manufacture their missing forms**. Hypothesis 4’s crowded fleet is visible, but its semantic/composition solution belongs outside this pipeline. Hypothesis 5 was not investigated here.
7. **Headroom is real, not transferable without measurement.** RTX baseline: total GPU p50 2.47/p95 3.17 ms, 179 calls, 374,708 triangles, 50 textures (`outputs/holistic/day.json:30–88,1699–1700`). Per-pass timings are not additive, especially on M5/ANGLE Metal (`docs/pharosville/TESTING.md:505–510`). Unresolved historical 566–575k triangle spikes preclude treating all nominal triangle headroom as available (`agents/2026-10-02-visual-upgrade/02-execution-record.md:81–87`).

## Ranked levers
### 1. One sky–irradiance–air lighting system
**Change:** Coordinate `garden-sky`, `garden-aerial`, `garden-day-cycle`, `garden-environment`: derive daytime sky radiance, PMREM and diffuse fill from one solar model; replace duplicated fill energy, retain bounded stale-source fog. Three’s installed WebGL [Sky addon](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Sky.html) provides Rayleigh/Mie/turbidity controls; disable its decorative cloud animation and preserve the PSI cloud ladder. Author a moon-independent low-frequency night bounce/readability floor on garden/ship surfaces, not global exposure or emissive water.
**Impact 5/5:** Clear noon recession and readable shaded/night surfaces improve the entire frame. **Effort L. Risk:** physical sky can wash silhouettes or dilute issuer colours. **Contracts:** atmosphere-before-grade, actual clock/PSI ownership (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:69–95`), anchors (`:114–122`), night-water ceiling (`CONTRACTS.md:378–382`). **Dependencies:** light/sky, water and material owners. **Verify:** matched noon/golden/blue/night previews, ninths/notan, tower shade ratio, night-water probe; day-cycle/sky/environment tests and M5 DPR1/2 timings.

### 2. Grounded garden shadow architecture
**Change:** In `renderer-shadow-rig`, implement contact-hardening for static garden casters with stable world-space filtering and texel-stable fits; keep cached redraw ownership. A custom WebGL2 PCSS implementation needs raw blocker depth in addition to comparison sampling—explicit resource/shader ownership, not an old global ShaderChunk paste. Use a localized screen-space contact-shadow pass only for moving near/selected vessels, sharing composer depth; distant fleet remains cheap. Start with one fitted map; cascades are justified only by measured near/far texel insufficiency.
**Impact 4/5:** Stones, eaves and pine branches touch their surroundings; soft penumbrae describe scale rather than toy silhouettes. **Effort L. Risk:** blocker-search noise, peter-panning, off-screen contact loss, shader coupling with shafts. **Contracts:** resource/GPU ceilings and tier transitions (`CONTRACTS.md:385–395`); one RAF/static frame (`:14–26`). **Dependencies:** redesigned terrain/vegetation and low-sun ray depth sampling. **Verify:** sunlit/shaded stone and branch crops, golden rest plus moving-ship bursts; shadow/resource/disposal tests, settled and re-steer GPU costs on M5.

### 3. Local indirect detail instead of stronger global AO
**Change:** Bake shared static AO/lightmap atlases for redesigned threshold stones, moss/eaves and precinct; use `MeshStandardMaterial` indirect-light inputs, with authored sky/ground bounce and instanced per-object exposure where appropriate. Retain N8AO for dynamic contacts, but remove double-occlusion on baked surfaces through an explicit scope policy. Keep Neutral initially; evaluate grade/AO knockouts before altering LUT contrast/vignette.
**Impact 4/5:** Material-scale crevices and sheltered bounce remain legible without blackening whole surfaces. **Effort M** after geometry settles. **Risk:** baked seams, UV/instance mismatch, doubling AO; day/night inconsistency if direct sun is baked. **Contracts:** textureless threshold rule must change (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:56–57`); 72-texture cap, anchors and calm blur stay. **Dependencies:** terrain/vegetation/material authoring. **Verify:** garden-wide rest improvement, not only macro crops; atlas/generator/resource tests, no-AO comparison, reduced-motion night and overview residency.

## Do-not-do / traps
- Keep 4×MSAA + DPR-gated SMAA: documented M5 comparison found both valuable (`garden-post.ts:1392–1434`). Three’s `TAARenderPass` has **no reprojection**, requires stationary scenes (`node_modules/three/examples/jsm/postprocessing/TAARenderPass.js:7–14`); it is unsuitable for bobbing ships. Velocity-aware custom TAA is an L-sized project, not first leverage.
- `three/addons/GTAOPass` is available at this version but uses the examples composer API (`GTAOPass.js:23–43`), requiring a pmndrs adapter. No evidence warrants replacing N8AO.
- [realism-effects](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/0beqz/realism-effects/main/package.json) advertises SSGI/temporal effects but pins Three `^0.151.3`: **not verified compatible with 0.186**. Do not prescribe it as drop-in SOTA. Prefer local baked indirect detail over SSR/SSGI history instability.
- Full-scene SSR/planar reflection violates hero-only scope (`CONTRACTS.md:372–377`); DOF hides analytical ships and strengthens miniature-diorama appearance [INFERENCE]. More god rays, bloom, or crease outlines spend attention without solving garden identity. AgX/ACES substitution cannot fix lighting; Neutral is an intentional chroma decision.
- WebGPU/TSL is unwarranted now: current pmndrs peer range includes 0.186 (`node_modules/postprocessing/package.json:70–72`), and migration conflicts with `CONTRACTS.md:16–18` plus the prior NO-GO (`THREEJS_AGENT_REFERENCE.md:13–18`).
- A2/B1/P1/W1 already failed whole-frame discrimination; avoid another micro-tuning packet (`02-execution-record.md:17–20`).

## Invariants worth challenging
Replace the **three smooth, textureless threshold draws** prescription (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:56–57`) with a bounded shared-atlas material allowance: it prohibits exactly the surface-scale grounding the prominent foreground needs. Preserve restraint, unpickability and measured budgets. Do not relax access, clock, colour or renderer boundaries. Treat old numerical value targets as composition evidence, not permission for unreadable night data.

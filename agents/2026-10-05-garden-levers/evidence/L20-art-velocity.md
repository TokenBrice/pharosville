# L20 — Codebase & tooling as enabler of visual iteration

## Verdict
The repo has unusually good **measurement tooling**, but no comparable artist-facing **authoring loop**: appearance is scattered through procedural factories, shader literals and composition code. The highest-value investment is a small production-scene lookdev surface plus reproducible whole-frame comparisons—not a renderer rewrite or another round of isolated coefficient tuning.

## Evidence
1. **Large files are integration hotspots, not proof that everything is monolithic.** `world-renderer.ts` is 4,053 lines, the route 1,553, and the loop 1,861 (read snapshots). Ten named content parts already have persistent roots, epochs and upload owners (`src/three/world-renderer.ts:1624-1648`), selective rebuilds (`:799-935`) and explicit build dispatch (`:2028-2085`). Semantic-view, ship-frame, shadow-rig and transition seams already exist (`docs/pharosville/THREEJS_AGENT_REFERENCE.md:48`). Nevertheless the 175-line `GardenContent` aggregate and centrally sequenced environmental/ritual/material updates make changing several visual systems a coordinator edit (`src/three/world-renderer.ts:1435-1608,3492-3581`). The route already delegates many concerns to hooks (`src/pharosville-world.tsx:16-44`); splitting it for line-count optics would not directly accelerate art.
2. **Useful knobs exist, but in different forms.** Lighting has five authored rigs and exposure tables (`src/three/garden-day-cycle.ts:131-209,220-228`); post has private beat tables (`src/three/garden-post.ts:127-165`); threshold layout and dyes are module constants (`src/three/garden-threshold.ts:72-112,175-210`); crag parameters are interpolated into GLSL strings (`src/three/garden-crag-finish.ts:29-46,74-95`). Shared palette, air and material-patch composition are real assets (`src/systems/palette.ts:42-91`; `src/three/garden-aerial.ts:39-42,372-386`), not missing infrastructure to reinvent.
3. **Current debugging observes the world; it does not author it.** The published seam contains camera/projected anchors, targets, samples, metrics and forced rituals, but no material/scene editing interface (`src/hooks/use-world-render-loop.ts:1314-1436`). Vite/React development support is configured (`package.json:20`; `vite.config.ts:121-128`), while the renderer is dynamically loaded and retained by an effect (`src/hooks/use-world-render-loop.ts:135,439-471`). [INFERENCE] Ordinary HMR is not evidence of safe in-place GPU-resource replacement: the retained renderer closure needs an explicit rebuild/rebind contract. Edit-to-visible latency was **not measured**. Preview defaults to six seconds' dwell; asserted animated runs add twelve seconds' tail, after fleet population and pacing settlement (`scripts/pharosville/preview.mjs:295-312,493-544`). Five 14-second asserted hour arms therefore impose 130 seconds of programmed dwell/tail alone, excluding launch/loading.
4. **Tests protect substantial correctness, but some protect the previous art recipe.** Last release recorded 2,218 passing tests, not 2,218 visual-quality judgments (`agents/2026-10-02-visual-upgrade/02-execution-record.md:13,31`). `scripts/check-guards.test.mjs:10-92,359-385` primarily covers operational/security/docs/workflow tooling, not garden aesthetics. Conversely the threshold test requires exactly three meshes, no material textures and one single-instance mesh (`src/three/garden-threshold.test.ts:197-223`) and preserves pad metadata/trunk while contouring only branch zero (`:226-249`). Useful water-clearance protection is separate (`:135-178`). `validate:changed` sends every non-doc change through full validation (`scripts/pharosville/validate-changed.mjs:71-74,144-145`), despite the smallest-check guidance (`docs/pharosville/TESTING.md:5-18`). [INFERENCE] Using that release lane for each exploratory look wastes feedback cycles.
5. **The visible limitation is not lack of elaborate code.** Day's lower bank is a broad soft green field and the near pine has blunt cloud pads; overview exposes the plate silhouette (`outputs/holistic/day.png`, bottom quarter/top-left; `overview.png`, outer perimeter). These support hypotheses 1–2, although the detailed tower refutes a uniformly low-detail description. The bible explicitly mandates texturelessness and inward-only pine contouring (`docs/pharosville/VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:47-57`): tooling alone cannot override that direction. A2/B1/P1 were rejected for indistinguishable/no whole-frame gain; W1 was unwarranted (`agents/2026-10-02-visual-upgrade/02-execution-record.md:17-20`).
6. **No dedicated editor was located; hidden copies are not a preset system.** Repo-wide searches found no GUI dependency, `import.meta.hot` handler or Three-devtools hook; the inspected debug surface is telemetry, not an inspector (finding 3). The hidden island/islet snapshots have no inbound references in the repository search and import retired modules (`src/three/.garden-island-g1.ts:51`; `src/three/.garden-islets-g1.ts:18-19`); glob searches found none of those targets. These are authoring debt, not shipped visual cost. Retire only after checking ownership/history; historical variants belong in worktrees, not unresolved source copies.

## Levers — ranked
### 1. Production-scene art controls · Impact 4/5 · Effort M
**Change:** Introduce one small typed appearance-config seam consumed by existing day-cycle/post/threshold owners; add a development-only lazy panel with coherent presets, reset and export. Start with light/air balance, landform profile and moss/stone response—not every literal. Classify changes as uniform-only, material-recompile or named-part rebuild; queue the latter through existing ownership/epoch machinery. HMR replaces config and requests the existing paint, never starts another RAF. Show dirty/rebuild status and the current preset identity.

**Why:** [INFERENCE] Turns serial code-edit/reload guesses into immediate, reversible whole-frame decisions; a preset must change shape/material as well as grade to avoid repeating A2.

**Risk/contracts:** Stale GPU handles and accidentally adjustable analytical encodings. Keep peg bands, issuer colours, data placement and wall-clock semantics outside the panel; preserve `THREEJS_AGENT_REFERENCE` §§Frame contract/Scene and resource discipline (`:75-90,162-174`) and `VISUAL_INVARIANTS` §§Atmosphere/Immutable colour anchors. Development GUI must be compile-time excluded from production, not merely hidden; the last release used 948/963 KiB gzip (`02-execution-record.md:31`).

**Dependencies/verification:** Lighting, terrain and material owners agree on a bounded config. Tests: config defaults, changed-part invalidation, disposal, static repaint, unchanged semantic world. Real-GPU preview: 20 repeated preset swaps with no accumulating textures/geometries, one RAF (zero at settled reduced motion), cold/warm preset image parity and unchanged data/targets.

### 2. Reproducible look-selection lane · Impact 5/5 · Effort S
**Change:** Extend `scripts/pharosville/preview.mjs` orchestration with serial baseline/candidate contact sheets using existing fixtures, fixed observer/date, rest camera and five hours. Reuse its capture manifest (`:762-789`), metrics/notan, draw census and blur audit; add preset identity/config checksum when lever 1 exists. Compare the whole image first, then one near-garden crop. Also retain live/dense/selected/constrained checks before acceptance.

**Why:** [INFERENCE] Faster rejection of invisible work and reliable choice between genuinely different treatments is more valuable than hundreds of controls.

**Risk/contracts:** Optimizing ninths MAE instead of making a garden. `TESTING.md` §§Visual review/Instruments (`:60-80,455-478`) already supports this lane; do not replace release gates. Dependencies: art direction chooses two or three distinct candidates. Verification: manifest identity matches across arms, cold recapture reproduces the winner, perceptible whole-frame gain, resource ceilings and Apple M5 Pro pacing; focused tests for comparison/manifest plumbing.

### 3. Extract by existing ownership, not by file length · Impact 3/5 · Effort M
**Change:** Move current content construction/disposal and environment-frame composition out of `world-renderer.ts` along its ten-part and scene-scope seams. Keep the root coordinating renderer/context/post and reflection order. Add a dev-only owner tree with isolate/highlight controls and census costs; name anonymous draws rather than build a generic scene editor.

**Why:** [INFERENCE] Lets terrain/material/atmosphere work avoid the same integration file and makes authored layer failures diagnosable. No wholesale route/loop split first.

**Risk/contracts:** Reflection, async model cancellation, cargo→dock/tender→ship dependencies (`world-renderer.ts:1620-1648`) and shared cache lifetime. Preserve runtime guide §§Frame contract/Resource discipline and all hard ceilings. Dependencies: lifecycle/renderer owner. Verification: existing renderer/loop focused tests, selection and repeated rebuild cleanup; real-GPU baseline pixel/metric parity before any new art.

### 4. Three reusable finish recipes · Impact 4/5 · Effort M
**Change:** Centralize only moss, stone and weathered timber appearance defaults, composing the existing aerial/flora/ink shader patches. Migrate the first near-garden/terrain users; keep materials subtree-owned rather than introducing a global disposable-material cache. Add a local lookdev specimen preset in the same runtime.

**Risk/contracts:** Shader patch order, instancing and skyline noise; requires explicit relaxation of threshold texturelessness if textures are used. `THREEJS_AGENT_REFERENCE` §§Media rules/Resource discipline and `VISUAL_INVARIANTS` §§Hierarchy/Colour anchors apply. Dependencies: stylization/terrain owners and local asset pipeline. Verification: focused shader/material tests, all-hour preview crops plus rest frames, texture census, no shimmer at overview.

### 5. Separate art hypotheses from safety gates · Impact 3/5 · Effort S
**Change:** Document a focused exploratory lane using existing targeted tests and preview; full validation stays at integration/release. Replace obsolete exact art-recipe pins only when new direction is accepted; retain semantic, clearance, budget and lifecycle assertions. Inventory hidden `.garden-*` variants before retiring them—never turn stale copies into a second runtime convention.

**Risk/contracts:** Accidentally weakening meaningful coverage. `CONTRACTS.md:10-12` already says old implementation pins are not picture authority. Dependencies: test/process owner. Verification: guard-routing unit tests and deliberately failing safety cases; accepted-direction GPU matrix, not weaker screenshots.

## Do-not-do / traps
No R3F/WebGPU migration, generic ECS/editor framework, all-literals registry, or allocation-heavy reactive state in RAF. Do not equate GUI sliders with art direction, tune only a flattering hour, or resurrect rejected micro-craft. Do not delete tests wholesale or claim this review timed HMR/builds.

## Invariants worth challenging
Challenge `VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:47-57`: six anchored inward-only pads plus three textureless threshold draws lock the exact silhouette/material regime implicated by the captures and rejected A2. Replace those implementation pins with authored framing, hull clearance, calm spatial-frequency limits and a measured local budget. Keep runtime/truth/gate/colour contracts. Treat numerical ninths as diagnostics, not the sole aesthetic objective (`:27-36`).

# L05 — Vegetation & garden elements

## Verdict
The garden has a surprisingly complete planting vocabulary in code, but its **solid cloud-pad grammar and textureless foreground erase botanical and material identity**. The largest gain is replacing the near-seat pine/lawn abstraction with a composed, materially differentiated garden threshold—not adding more plants or Japanese props.

## Evidence
1. **H1/H2 confirmed visually, with an important qualification.** `outputs/holistic/day.png` (upper-left bough, bottom third) shows thick angular arms ending in solid mushroom discs over a broad, smooth green bank. `day-1200x640.png` repeats that reading; `day-900x720.png` almost loses the bough to cropping. The frame has pines and a tōrō, but moss, stepping stones, gravel, maple, bamboo and grass are not independently legible. This is not simply “flat shading”: threshold materials explicitly use `flatShading: false` (`src/three/garden-threshold.ts:804–828`). Smooth primitive shading still reads as a toy.
2. **Pine structure is thoughtful; foliage closure is the bottleneck.** `src/three/garden-niwaki.ts:173–207,232–287,304–374` builds plated tapered tubes, root flares, upturned arms and flattened 3–5-icosphere pads. UVs are removed; bark plates and needle undersides are vertex-colour ramps. The near threshold merges two authored trees into one instanced draw (`garden-threshold.ts:820–836`). These are authored silhouettes already: another random perturbation is not an authored-asset step change.
3. **Species differences collapse into one silhouette vocabulary.** Maple/cherry literally call `createNiwakiPine` (`src/three/garden-flora.ts:249–261`); bamboo has eleven five-sided culms under four solid tall lobes (`:264–320`); karikomi uses three clipped icosphere lobes (`:323–357`). All species share one smooth, very rough standard-material construction (`:439–469`). Existing placement is restrained: five rim maples, three cherries intentionally off the rest frame, two bamboo groves, chained low shrubs (`src/three/garden-rim-mesh.ts:940–962,984–992`). **Refute the hypothesis that garden vocabulary is wholly absent; confirm that it is unreadable at rest.**
4. **Stone/gravel vocabulary exists but is poorly allocated to the hero view.** The threshold already has a stone triad and five stepping stones, each tiny displaced icospheres (`src/three/garden-threshold.ts:365–397`). Islets have intentional upright/subordinate/low-back groupings, but share displaced detail-1 icospheres (`src/three/garden-islets.ts:124–185`). More convincing broad-based, buried, bedded forms exist in `src/three/garden-set-stones.ts:20–85`. The actual karesansui bed is explicitly **outside the rest frame**; its rake changes diffuse value by 14%, not normals/displacement (`src/three/garden-stone-garden.ts:29–47,147–176`). Tsukubai and fine grasses do not read in the captures; adding either is not automatically the right fix.
5. **Season/motion foundations are strong; representation is weak.** Continuous seeded phenology and hemisphere-aware kō already exist (`src/systems/garden-calendar.ts:3–17,158–185`). Crown loss collapses whole pads, snow whitens upward foliage (`garden-flora.ts:145–195`); seasonal leaves/petals are untextured rectangular planes (`src/three/garden-seasonal-dressing.ts:214–224,295–299`). Wind bends the whole height profile using shared weather; the threshold attenuates its sway (`garden-flora.ts:477–538`; `garden-threshold.ts:835–836`). Static captures cannot establish wind quality or seasonal transitions. `reduced.png` confirms the same material/silhouette limitations without motion. H3's near-black foreground is confirmed in `night.png`; glowing plants would be the wrong cure.
6. **Headroom is real, not unlimited.** Noon records 374,708 triangles/50 textures and GPU p95 3.17 ms; overview records 43 textures (`outputs/holistic/day.json:55–56,1699–1700`; `overview.json:1628–1629`). The asset document's “72/72” statement is historical, not today's measurement. Retain the tighter 480k-triangle/60-texture acceptance and measured owner deltas (`docs/pharosville/CONTRACTS.md:385–395`); the previous release also records an unresolved long-session triangle spike (`agents/2026-10-02-visual-upgrade/02-execution-record.md:81–87`). RTX headroom is not M5 proof.

## Ranked levers

### 1. Hero niwaki, not cloud caps
**Change:** Replace threshold pine pads/arms with two reference-authored tapered skeletons, secondary twigs and irregular, porous needle-cluster silhouettes; carry a smaller exemplar onto the lighthouse's left headland. Prefer opaque clustered meshes. Local baked geometry or checked assets should replace the old hero recipes, while distant planting retains cheap silhouettes. **Impact 5/5:** fixes the immediately recognizable “mushroom tree” at the frame entrance. **Effort M. Risk:** over-detailed silhouette, sky occlusion, shadow cost. **Touched:** challenge exact six-pad/inward-only recipe and 15k micro-budget (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:47–57`); preserve shaded corner and tower hierarchy (`:13–25,40–45`). Checked GLBs require explicit ownership/provenance amendment (`CONTRACTS.md:399–403`; `ASSET_PIPELINE.md:25–46,87–96`). **Dependencies:** composition, light/shadow, asset pipeline. **Verification:** matched whole-frame/upper-left crops at all gate profiles and phases on real-GPU preview; niwaki/threshold tests updated to visual-role rather than exact old pad geometry.

### 2. One moss–stone–gravel threshold composition
**Change:** With terrain ownership, replace part of the smooth bank with two asymmetric low moss islands, one recessed gravel/earth channel and a legible half-buried stone triad; re-seat the existing stepping stones into the channel. Use authored substrate masks, world-space moss coverage, roughness/normal variation and filtered rake normals. Put richness in lower-left/lower-centre **land**, never across the inlet. No grass carpet; one tiny grass/reed edge patch only if it replaces existing dressing. **Impact 5/5:** turns the largest non-data area into a garden through material relationships and space. **Effort L. Risk:** visual busyness, texture aliasing, exposed plate edge. **Touched:** relax “textureless/three draws” (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:52–57`); retain broad shaded planes, blur calm region, global caps (`CONTRACTS.md:383–395`). **Dependencies:** L06 terrain heightfield/placement masks, palette and lighting. **Verification:** preview notan/value-plan/temporal metrics plus full-size review; threshold/rim tests retain coverage and placement safety.

### 3. Distinct species, shared rendering infrastructure
**Change:** Replace maple/cherry pine-pad recipes with fine bifurcating branches and thin leaf clusters; bamboo with visible segmented culms and drooping sprays. Keep karikomi as restrained contiguous clipped masses, but remove obvious bead seams. Reuse batches, seasonal attributes and shared weather; move one existing maple into a readable left-margin position, bamboo behind the left station rather than a new vertical monument. Bounded sun/moon-driven backlighting can suggest leaf translucency without emission or physical transmission. **Impact 4/5:** establishes botanical variety and softness without larger counts. **Effort L. Risk:** alpha overdraw/AO halos. **Touched:** palette primacy (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:114–122`), media batching/static state (`CONTRACTS.md:404–419`). **Dependencies:** pipeline, shadow deformation, flora placement. **Verification:** species/phenology tests; golden-hour burst and reduced captures. Alpha-cutout cards/impostors are conditional: prove matched depth, shadow and N8AO behavior first—the current islet code explicitly warns against cards (`garden-islets.ts:219–221`).

### 4. Author stone faces and make raking tangible
**Change:** Replace threshold/islet noisy spheres with a small authored rock set: upright, reclining and flat stones sharing coherent bedding, selected hard edges, buried contact and moss/wet masks. Give the existing grave-bed rake derivative-filtered normal relief rather than only stripes. Preserve grave family forms, cap-based sizes and ledger anchors; decorative triads must not masquerade as asset counts. **Impact 4/5:** credible ishigumi and tactile ground, especially once lever 2 makes them visible. **Effort M. Risk:** polished/scanned realism mismatch, moiré. **Touched:** analytical parity (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:59–67`), material/asset ownership and global budgets. **Dependencies:** terrain seating, selected-grave interaction. **Verification:** stone-garden/islet tests, close and overview GPU captures, grave selection unchanged.

### 5. Season readable before the ritual
**Change:** Preserve calendar/score; represent leaf loss in leaf clusters, retain credible bare branch architecture, and replace seasonal rectangles with shaped leaf/petal geometry. Ensure one existing near-view specimen communicates spring/autumn in the static frame; do not multiply particle counts or ritual frequency. **Impact 3/5:** a living garden during ordinary visits, not only a rare animation. **Effort M. Risk:** seasonal foliage competing with analytical colour. **Touched:** kō/attention, immutable colours and complete static state (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:97–122`; `CONTRACTS.md:415–419`). **Dependencies:** lever 3, calendar/director. **Verification:** calendar/seasonal-dressing tests; dated normal/reduced captures and one forced leaf-fall burst.

## Do-not-do / traps
- No prop checklist: torii, extra lanterns, dense bamboo walls, grass everywhere or a decorative tsukubai cannot repair spatial composition (`PRODUCT.md:41–42`).
- No extra wind to manufacture richness; do not brighten foliage with emission at night.
- No unproven transparent foliage, per-tree textures, new runtime packages, remote assets or gate-bypassing loads.
- A2 silhouette micro-craft and P1 grove craft were rejected as indistinguishable; W1 motion tuning was unwarranted (`02-execution-record.md:17–20`). Judge visible whole-frame differences, not source complexity.

## Invariants worth challenging
Challenge **implementation-shaped** threshold rules—six exact pads, inward-only edits, texturelessness, three draws, 15k triangles—not the garden's shade/negative-space role (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:47–57`). Permit explicit checked vegetation/stone media ownership where procedural primitives cannot supply the required silhouette. Preserve global budgets, same-origin assets, desktop gate, DOM truth, shared clock and static reduced motion. Re-measure overview textures before funding an atlas; do not claim the historical 72/72 census blocks every material upgrade.

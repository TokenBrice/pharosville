# L12 — Motion, ambience, life, sound

## Verdict
The gap is **perceptible garden life at the resting seat**, not a missing animation engine: the shared wind, water and audio systems are already unusually coherent. The scene spends visible motion on ships and harbour water while garden life is small, peripheral or narrowly scheduled; [INFERENCE] richer near-field responses and acoustics would feel more alive without increasing event frequency. Static captures cannot establish whether the experience actually feels mechanical; no temporal footage or audio was auditioned in this review.

## Evidence
1. **The garden foreground is visually inert-looking, not proven motionless.** `outputs/holistic/day.png` (bottom third and upper-left pine) shows broad smooth land planes and solid crown pads; no koi habitat is legible from this seat. This confirms hypothesis 2's featureless-bank observation and partly supports hypothesis 1's weak garden vocabulary. Yet the threshold pines already have wind sway (`src/three/garden-threshold.ts:820-830,867-869`). The hook bends vertices by height using one material-wide strength (`src/three/garden-flora.ts:515-517,534-537`): it does not itself sample the travelling gust at each plant position. [INFERENCE] deforming whole pads cannot deliver the layered, locally delayed response of twigs, needles and grasses.
2. **Ambient foundations exist; do not replace them.** The weather plan provides a 9-second breath, a 600-second gust with 12-second attack/36-second release, spatial front travel at 6 units/second, and slow wind wander (`src/systems/weather.ts:66-88,131-157,215-230`). Hulls sample the actual seven-component water field, not arbitrary synchronized bob (`src/three/garden-hull-swell.ts:12-20,83-90`). Clouds have a 90-second market-band transition by contract (`docs/pharosville/VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:90-95`). A new wind manager would duplicate infrastructure.
3. **Life exists but is not guaranteed to be readable.** Koi have continuous figure-eight/station-loop paths but fade with daylight (`src/three/garden-koi.ts:77-109,269-272`); heron strikes generate a local ring (`src/three/garden-heron.ts:591-605`), whereas arrival/departure and dawn skein are narrow solar/seasonal score windows (`src/systems/garden-score.ts:211-225,251-257`). `outputs/holistic/night.png` (near garden) confirms hypothesis 3's near-black foreground: movement cannot rescue unseen subjects. Fauna placement, light and camera are dependencies, not just cadence knobs.
4. **Zero admissions do not prove an over-strict director.** Both 30-minute watches had zero admissions and 17.1% visible hulls underway, but lacked a ritual window; no crossing qualified, and both recordings failed (`agents/2026-10-02-visual-upgrade/02-execution-record.md:54-65`). Fish rings deliberately avoid solar noon ±2 hours (`src/systems/garden-score.ts:118-128`). More importantly, **natural fish rings are unreachable**: generated entries have `windowSec: 0` (`:452-458`), while the driver skips `clockSec >= startSec + windowSec` before requiring `clockSec >= startSec` (`:655-661`). Forced captures bypass this failure. Existing ring tests inspect generated placement, while the driver example uses a 240-second window (`src/systems/garden-score.test.ts:53-56,100-121`). Long visits additionally thin decorative gifts toward 35% (`garden-score.ts:599-607`).
5. **Fleet cadence is already restrained and technically credible.** Voyages use 90–180-second legs/600–1500-second rests; only a qualified crossing receives the foreground caption (`docs/pharosville/CONTRACTS.md:410-414,447-454`). Display damping is 0.10–0.18 seconds, with explicit discontinuity handling (`src/systems/visual-motion.ts:52-58,158-176`). Hypothesis 4's dense sails is visible in `day.png` (mid-frame), but changing these smoothing constants is not a composition fix, and stills cannot judge cadence.
6. **Sound is capable but harbour-centric.** Consent, lazy loading and separate Music preference are sound design foundations (`src/hooks/use-garden-sound.ts:70-76`). The bed uses filtered noise for sea/wash/wind, shared gust stereo travel and stone laps (`src/lib/pharosville-audio/bed.ts:82-89,164-188`). A sparse generative pentatonic mallet/pluck score already exists, 40–90 seconds between phrases, muted during rituals and early night (`music.ts:1-14,31-34,173-187`). Distant buoy/rocks occur no sooner than 3–6 minutes after consent, then 10–16 minutes apart (`pharosville-audio.ts:44-46`; `borrowed.ts:29-32,96-98`). No garden basin/foliage acoustic stem exists in the mix (`mix.ts:18-43`). This is a palette/physical-source opportunity, not evidence that the current mix sounds bad.

## Ranked levers
### 1. Rest-seat living materiality
**Change:** In `garden-threshold.ts`/`garden-flora.ts`, author trunk/branch/tip flex weights and spatially delayed shared-gust response; make existing planting read as layered needles/twigs, with a small reed/grass margin replacing smooth bank surface. Couple wet-edge ripples and near foliage rustle to that same field; keep the distant harbour quieter.

**Impact 5/5:** Makes the actual viewing seat recognizably alive rather than merely increasing remote animation. **Effort M. Risk:** wobbling toy crowns, aliasing, extra detail competing with the tower. **Touched:** threshold three-draw/15k-triangle/textureless rule (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:47-57`), displacement and continuous-motion rules (`:99-112`), renderer ceilings (`ARCHITECTURE.md:90-95`). **Dependencies:** vegetation geometry, terrain/water edge, lighting. **Verify:** real-GPU 90-second rest-seat sequences at noon/night and both gate profiles; foliage masks and wavefront ordering, not whole-frame activity alone; static determinism, shader and resource checks.

### 2. Readable resident ecology, reliable scored gifts
**Change:** Repair the zero-width fish-ring admission at its source, with a bounded non-replaying start window. Recompose existing koi habitat/heron station into a clear garden sightline; replace perpetual geometric koi loops with slow travel/pause/turn states sampled deterministically from the same clock. Preserve populations and solar arrivals; improve present-state habitat rather than replaying missed rituals on entry.

**Impact 5/5:** A visitor can discover living residents even when no foreground gift is due. **Effort M. Risk:** apparent choreography or making creatures analytical symbols. **Touched:** fauna-count/displacement ban and ritual ownership (`CONTRACTS.md:463-477`); quiet budget unchanged (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:105-112`). **Dependencies:** camera, pond/terrain, daylight visibility. **Verify:** generated fish-ring entry naturally admitted once after initial silence, before/after-window and hidden-resume tests; matched 30-minute natural/forced captures; unchanged budget proof and complete reduced-motion habitat.

### 3. A garden acoustic foreground, not another soundtrack
**Change:** Extend `bed.ts`, `engine.ts`, `scene-snapshot.ts` and `mix.ts` with one audible, visible hydraulic source: basin trickle and occasional suikinkutsu-like resonances, plus foliage-specific rustle. Replace some existing shore/lap energy rather than summing more hiss. Use modal synthesis or a few self-hosted recordings; spatialize by source/listener and apply modest shelter filtering. Keep the existing music generator. A shishi-odoshi knock, if used, must occupy an admitted event and displace a far cue—not become a minute metronome. Do not sonify mint/burn direction or turn distress into alarms; any sea-state timbre remains the already-labelled water reading.

**Impact 4/5:** Establishes intimate listening distance and garden identity for consenting visitors. **Effort M. Risk:** sonic costume, synthetic timbre, startling transients. **Touched:** opt-in/no-market-alarm contract (`CONTRACTS.md:478-484`), master −6 dBTP ceiling/six voices (`mix.ts:45-81`), runtime-local media/bundle budgets (`TESTING.md:90-103`). **Dependencies:** physical basin, near-field geometry, source snapshot. **Verify:** headphone/speaker audition plus stem/peak reports; consent/hidden/resume tests, Still with independently consented sound, no audio requests before consent.

### 4. Accept temporal experiences, not quietness statistics
**Change:** Require saved natural-motion recordings and listening evidence alongside forced ritual plates. Keep regional salience, event counts and quiet spans separate. The offline audio recorder is an audition, not a natural session: it forces Music and inserts every borrowed sound/beat (`record.ts:103-128`).

**Impact 3/5:** Prevents repeating "zero admissions = calm" or "forced ritual works = score works." **Effort S. Risk:** capture tooling overhead. **Touched:** evidence interpretation (`TESTING.md:487-493`); no runtime invariant changes. **Dependencies:** orchestrator's serialized capture lane. **Verify:** playable recordings, advancing observer clock, actual admitted/visible subject linkage, Apple M5 Pro pacing; existing targeted motion/audio tests.

## Do-not-do / traps
- Do not raise fauna counts, fill the inlet, add camera touring, or autoplay sound.
- Do not retune damping or inflate global sway/bloom to manufacture life. A2/B1/P1 craft was rejected as indistinguishable; W1 showed tiny temporal deltas, not a missing motion system (`02-execution-record.md:17-20`).
- Do not treat RTX headroom as attention permission. Preserve exact DOM truth, same-origin API boundaries and the desktop gate.

## Invariants worth challenging
Challenge the **textureless, exactly three-draw threshold** (`VISUAL_INVARIANTS.md:56-57`): a modest batched needle/grass/wet-stone layer could materially improve tactile life without changing hierarchy. Do **not** relax six events/hour or 12-minute quiet first: continuous life is already exempt. Reconsider noon's four-hour fish-ring blackout and long-session gift thinning only after natural evidence; source logic already explains scarcity.

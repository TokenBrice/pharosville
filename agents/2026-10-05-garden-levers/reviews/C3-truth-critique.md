## Verdict
The direction is sound, and S3/S5/S6/S8 contain unusually good truth safeguards. The consolidated plan is not yet safe to authorize: its early release boundary precedes the teaching that makes the new water truthful, and its charter can be read as replacing rather than preserving the full analytical contract. Approve only after the following amendments.

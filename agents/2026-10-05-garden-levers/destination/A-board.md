## The picture

Sit beneath an irregular, branching black pine beside the cropped edge of weathered engawa boards. Three unequal, half-buried stones interrupt broad velvety moss shelves; a shaded stepping approach disappears behind the dominant stone before opening onto still water. One recessed gravel interval holds a quiet, dated PSI record rather than becoming a second dashboard. Across the continuous inlet, the weathered Pharos rises right of centre above its asymmetric geological headland. A few legible near vessels lead three unequal flotillas; smaller inventory recedes into the harbour rather than becoming a sail barcode. Neutral daylight reveals damp stone contacts and directional timber. At night, cool indigo fill separates the same garden, water and headland planes; the beacon is the singular light, not a pretext for luminous grass or more lamps. Garden recognition precedes harbour recognition without displacing the tower as the frame's hero.

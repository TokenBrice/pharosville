## Contract and budget

Preserve D11, on-demand analytical text, one boat per coin, existing supply/peg/circuit mappings, fog = stale retained data only, opt-in/off-by-default sound, sorted screen **and window** 720×600 gate, and one WebGL2 engine. Decoration uses calendar/clock; styling an existing weather/sea cue must preserve its declared input. Reduced motion freezes new movement; missing/unavailable readings never gain a reassuring pose. No API payload/schema/endpoint changes. Blender art remains Opus-authored under D4.

Adopt B-performance-program B01 measurement/replay protocol, B02/B03 feature preparation, B07 defined shader arithmetic, B13 shared preparation queue, and B08–B11 savings. E06 **is B20**, not another LUT implementation. O3-I8's shared wave evaluation/target reductions belong to B06/B09/B11; additional sea-shadow downsampling/view-fitted wakes are not presumed savings.

Costs are engineering estimates, **not measurements**. `C0` means Δdraws=0, Δtriangles=0, Δshader ALU=0, Δtexture bytes=0, Δmain-thread CPU≈0 ms/frame. Rows specify deviations; FS/VS ALU apply only to affected fragments/vertices. A=all tiers; M+=medium/high, low=C0; H=high only, low/medium=C0. CPU overhead of uniform-only rows is estimated <0.01 ms/frame. Audio rows cost zero GPU/texture work; PCM is stated separately. Every row's final risk includes iGPU risk.

No additive feature ships while its target tier fails B01. Low must be net non-regressing; M+ additions together reserve ≤0.5 ms GPU, H additions ≤1 ms GPU, **and** total GPU p95≤13 ms/end-to-end p95≤16.7 ms on physical Apple Silicon Safari 26 + Chrome. These are proposed allocation ceilings, not extra allowance above D14. Historical Raphael low@1 at 68–76 ms precludes claiming headroom. Measure matched A/B with instrumentation off too; never add subsystem savings together. Actual peak target allocations, uploads and CPU4 long tasks are acceptance evidence.

## The three contested designs

**Shadow decision:** B exclusion E12 remains for S22-I8's second depth map/copy. E05 instead extends existing SunRig invalidation. Registry reports active dynamic casters and static generation, finalized **after** caster updates, before render (SunRig currently precedes fleet). Fleet/folk/workers use opaque depth shadows near; crossfade over .85–1× existing 640/520/360 m fleet limits to cheap contact quads. Fade depth contribution only inside transition; stable world-space dither, never temporal noise. Beyond320 m figures use foot blobs. Reuse existing water contact stamps where sufficient, avoiding doubled darkening. Cached static shadows have no per-hull silhouette at landing.

Invalidate on light-fit/sun drift (.002 rad), map-size/tier, static geometry/season/discard, office-work placements, shipyard/wreck install, launch/tow and actor membership changes. Audit every `castShadow` owner. Sway already stays static in shadow twin (`world/materials.ts:855`), unlike predepth; preserve that contract. Dynamic presence keeps tier cadence. Sea-mask resolve waits for matching completed map/matrix; low skips via B06. Unknown caster activity fails to live refresh. Recorded O2 saving .41/2.37ms=17.6% is an **upper bound at that RTX view**, not an iGPU promise; allow no saving in transition/close views. New work is L, not O2's S.

**LUT decision:** choose row slicing, correct B20's 1–5ms estimate to O2's reported19.6ms bake. GPU bake's32 tiny layer submissions, new capability path and parity surface are unjustified before sliced CPU+upload measurement. Preserve equations and cadence; do not restart continually during easing. Accept previous complete grade until next complete generation, target≤250ms latency under CPU4. If missed, profile before reopening GPU bake.

**Mirror decision:** E07 ships independently first. E35 explicitly enables `beam || shimmer || mirror`; verify far-day mirror still renders with shimmer=0. Use opaque water alpha .05–.5 as mask, other surfaces1; preserve through fog and restore final alpha1. Reflect about mean water-plane normal; apply wave distortion at composite only. Depth raymarch14 steps+4 refinements, thickness/miss/edge fades, sea-clock streaking; preserve stale optical extinction. No normal attachment/G-buffer; reuse B03 preparation recipes, restore targets on cancellation, dispose targets on tier exit. Reject transparent-edge artifacts. **DECISION-NEEDED:** recommend high-only experiment, default off until operator and hardware acceptance. This reopens a deferred reflection with a different screen-space implementation, not approval for Medium. O3's .2–.4ms estimate omitted resurrected daytime bandwidth: composite alone reads/writes25.6MB/frame@1,57.6MB@1.5, plus SSR/depth traffic. Charge both passes; no subtraction of E07 savings to hide them.

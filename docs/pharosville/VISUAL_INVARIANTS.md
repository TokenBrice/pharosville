## The picture

The target is **A — Moss-side observatory**: an inhabited, shaded Japanese
viewing garden that is also a stablecoin-market observatory, in refined stylized
PBR. Sit beside weathered engawa boards beneath an irregular, porous black pine.
Broad moss shelves, three unequal embedded stones, a recessed gravel interval
and a stepping approach that disappears behind stone replace the smooth bank
and disc-pine silhouette. Garden space is recognizable before the harbour;
surface detail supports those masses rather than decorating every pixel.

A weathered Pharos remains the singular hero, slightly right of centre on an
asymmetric moss-and-stone headland: two unequal inclined shoulders answer one
dominant exposed oblique rock face beneath a level court. Interrupted planted
pockets replace circumferential moss terraces; the pond shelf, gate spur and
grounded stair causeway remain subordinate. Its broken reflection remains recognizable:
inverted tiers, a few irregular surface-axis strips and sharp rooted contact in
the continuous calm inlet. Keep the whole tracked fleet: a leading near-left group, quieter
rear-left crescent and receding right group have clear water between them.
A few near boats read as rigged vessels; the many beyond recede into silhouette.
Ships belong to the landscape rather than competing with its scale.

The six Stroll stations show the same accepted shore, sheltered tea veranda and
grounded precinct stair from inhabited viewpoints. Their titles, Previous/Next
and Home are navigation, never new analytical readings. Local inspection keeps
the station context; only Home returns to the seated composition.

Branching near-corner framing reveals sky through its foliage, never solid
cloud-pad discs. Borrowed headlands and a real sky band close the distance.
Use long-lens perspective to give the harbour recession and tower crown air.
Make the precinct a shoin court, engawa and dry-stone garden, not a fort.
The chaseki has a dark, usable seated-facing veranda, recessed plaster bays,
boarded threshold and a thick quiet hip/thatch eave, not a wall box or parasol.

the existing crown strip; neither shelter adds a monument or competes with the
tower, and both read through construction shadow in the static frame.
The pavilion, pond and signal mast remain subordinate to the tower; do not
add another monument. Neutral daylight reveals moss, damp stone and worked
timber; cool night fill separates the same planes without luminous lawn or
added lamps. The beacon remains dominant.

Near coastal scenery is anchored in the world: bounded four-sided decorative
land and its near headland show parallax when the visitor moves, rather than
following the eye like a painted backdrop. Four distant sky ranges and kasumi
remain eye-relative; three ranges retain their PSI visibility, the anchor peak
stays PSI-independent, and the tower crown keeps its sky gap.

## Atmosphere before grade

Build atmosphere from geometry and light before grade. Noon is neutral-white
and blue-green, not honey; golden hour rakes; blue hour is gentle. Let real sky,
borrowed hills, shadows and reflection establish depth before post-processing.
Supporting surface pigments are restrained: dark olive moss, mineral earth and
weathered roofs remain subordinate to the tower/inlet hierarchy. Their value
ladders survive greyscale and night fill; added saturation or a warm grade is
not a substitute for shaped light. Daytime sky/key radiance is S4-owned, not
the supporting palette, and no compensating LUT warmth restores a honey noon.
One analytic linear-radiance Rayleigh/Mie transport owns the daytime dome,
finite-distance object/water air, reflections and probe irradiance. It uses no

far-plate wash. At clear noon the authored 140/300-unit near/mid samples retain
≥0.9/0.8 luminance transmittance; greater distance and worsening displayed
accepted PSI increase recession smoothly, concentrated in borrowed hills.
The dome and air share the same horizon radiance without a water/sky seam.

hysteresis, never a flat-midday

scattering height, never imposed as sea-level solar extinction over the whole
dome. Clouds receive cosine-weighted sky irradiance plus projected direct light,
not the forward aerosol horizon radiance used as a surface pigment. Dawn/golden

The unscattered twilight basis stays separate from published analytic horizon

## Motion and restraint

Arrival reveals the complete rest composition as soon as data, warmup and motion
preference are ready. The branded first-byte gradient teaches only the generic
key and provides analytics links; it is not a live market picture. No spatial
intro rise or added optical depth survives readiness, and explicit URL intent
is never reset by arrival. This implementation uses an immediate cut (zero veil
settling), so neither motion preference nor input adds a waiting interval. The
separate admitted crossing ceremony remains an event, not an introduction.

Stroll advances only on request and rests indefinitely at each station. Adjacent
views follow explicit water-corridor and stair waypoints on piecewise smooth
eye/target paths, not a generic flyover arc. At ≤0.5-unit samples the eye clears
water by ≥1.7 and production land/massing by ≥0.8 world units. Drag/wheel retain
the displayed viewpoint locally without a grabbed-point snap; unrelated chrome
does not expel the visitor. Selection saves that local pose; Escape/deselection
restores it, while Home returns to the seat. Interruption holds the displayed
pose, resize re-solves it, and reduced motion cuts with no automatic tour/orbit.

Motion has long rests. Let an arrival, kindling lamps or a heron become an event,
then leave the garden quiet. Redistribute ambient life into fewer readable
figures, never add counts to manufacture life. Every addition names what it
displaces: a light demotes a light, a prop removes a prop, a motion stills another.
Cheap rendering does not make attention free.

Resident life stays at four koi and one heron. Pond inhabitants travel between
seeded near-bank points, pause completely and turn in place before travelling
again; no synchronized figure-eight or small station orbit substitutes for
rest. The tea/rest sightlines are composed through pond paths and the shallow
heron station, not brighter animals. Scored heron arrival/departure remain
events. Merged near foliage selects its baked root and trunk/branch/tip flex,
receiving two delayed samples of the one downwind gust front at cached world
rest roots; the root holds while branch and tip yield more. No per-vertex
uploads or second clock. Reduced motion fixes the complete animal pose and
zeros the complete foliage displacement.

The attention budget is a gate, enforced when the director admits an event
(`src/systems/garden-director.ts`) and proved for the day score
(`gardenScoreBudgetViolations`, `src/systems/garden-score.ts`). In any idle
hour: at most six discrete events, one unbroken 12-minute quiet, at least
8 minutes between foreground events and a 90 s back-off after any ritual. Over
a day: at most six gifts, no more than two between golden 0.5 and night 0.5.
Only the crossing subject crosses the inlet, and it wears the only nameplate.
Continuous ambient motion (wind, swell, water) is outside the count.

# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PharosVille serves stablecoin researchers, DeFi participants, risk analysts, and
curious observers who want to understand the market without beginning in a
dense table. Visitors may actively inspect one stablecoin or chain, or leave the
world running as a calm ambient view that gradually teaches the system.

## Product Purpose

PharosVille turns live Pharos stablecoin signals into an explorable maritime
observatory. It should make fleet-wide stability, peg-risk water, chain supply,
active stablecoins, and failed assets understandable through a coherent world,
while preserving exact values, provenance, caveats, and analytical depth in DOM
details.

Success means a visitor can first read the market's broad condition at a glance,
then understand why it looks that way, and finally inspect the underlying facts
without treating scenery or motion as financial truth.

## Garden Observatory outcome rubric (2026-10-05)

Direction A — Moss-side observatory — uses refined stylized PBR to make the
viewing garden recognizable before the harbour, while keeping the Pharos,
risk waters and supply leaders readable. Judge outcomes separately on a 1–5
scale; visual richness never substitutes for analytical truth:

- **Garden recognition:** an unprompted visitor recognizes an inhabited
  Japanese viewing garden through spatial structure, not a catalogue of props.
- **Relaxation:** the whole-frame hierarchy and quiet intervals remain pleasant
  during a ten-minute watch, at real day and night hours.
- **Market reading:** after a five-second aggregate/risk/leader reading, teaching
  through the key supports the correct interpretation and inspection of the
  exact dated record.

Acceptance requires operator preference, garden recognition and relaxation
≥4, market reading ≥3 after teaching, and zero confident false analytical
claims. Review randomized full frames before explanation, then blur/notan and
crops; record prefer/reject/undecided with reasons. MAE is diagnostic deviation
evidence only, never aesthetic approval. Models do not substitute for the
operator. Taste checkpoints are destination and CP-Final only; the
orchestrator archives autonomous destination review for final operator review.
The reading test and hardware evidence remain pending until actually gathered.

## Brand Personality

Contemplative, precise, and poetic. The experience should feel smooth and
appeasing enough to watch, with the spatial restraint and composed attention of
a Japanese garden, the life of a video game world, and the trustworthiness of a
serious analytics product.

## Anti-references

- Generic crypto dashboards dominated by charts, neon, badges, or trading
  urgency.
- Technology demos that use visual spectacle, photorealism, or camera freedom
  without improving understanding.
- Fantasy-village or ClaudeVille imitation, decorative lore, agent roleplay,
  and unrelated game mechanics.
- Dense scenery whose cultural references are literal decoration rather than
  principles of space, rhythm, season, and attention.
- WebGL- or 3D-only analytical truth that cannot be inspected, read by
  assistive technology, or understood with motion disabled.

## Design Principles

1. Data truth before spectacle: every analytical cue has a precise source,
   explanation, and DOM equivalent.
2. Calm reveals complexity: preserve negative space, progressive disclosure,
   and a clear visual hierarchy even when the underlying dataset is dense.
3. Teach through observation: motion, camera framing, and environmental change
   should help visitors notice and learn, not merely decorate the scene.
4. Keep the world coherent: one geography, one motion clock, stable semantics,
   and predictable interaction make the observatory trustworthy.
5. Earn graphical richness: add depth, lighting, materials, and effects only
   when they maintain smooth frame pacing and analytical legibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, readable detail panels, a complete accessibility
ledger, text alternatives for all analytical encodings, high-contrast UI, and
meaningful reduced-motion static states. The world runtime remains
desktop-gated, but the fallback must provide useful DOM links and must avoid
world data or graphics work. Color, depth, animation, sound, and 3D occlusion
must never be the only carrier of meaning.
